# Code of conduct

Unlease follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/): be welcoming, kind and constructive, in issues, pull requests, reviews and anywhere you speak for the project. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted, whoever they come from.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer: open a private report from the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), which only the maintainer sees, and say it is about conduct. Every report gets an answer and stays confidential. The maintainer may then ask for a correction, remove content, or ban someone from the project, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
