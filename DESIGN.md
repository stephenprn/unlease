# Design System: Carnet

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Galet et feuille: calm sheets resting on pebbles"**

Carnet is a notebook of calm paper sheets resting on pebbles, after its own mark (a beige card on a matcha pebble). Every container is a smooth sheet with one continuous corner, the same for every item of a role and at every size, so a hundred notes read as one quiet wall. The brand's pebble is the one organic form: it marks what is chosen, captured or kept (the capture pebble, the chosen place, the open info, a collection's emblem on its stone, a picture glimpsed), and it never holds text. The palette is habits-garden's: paper ground, cream sheets, warm near-black ink, terracotta fills, a matcha accent and fourteen earth pigments. That is Carnet's own look; on each device a person may choose another pair of colours (a palette, or each picked precisely) and another ground (a preset, a colour, or a gradient they generate, grainy or not), always fitted so every ratio of the system still holds (`design/appearance.ts`).

Density is Keep's: two columns on the phone, as many as fit on the desktop, each card exactly as tall as its content. One humanist face (Signika) at fixed sizes, weight carrying rank. Depth is paper resting on paper: a hairline edge, one soft light, quiet grain never stacked, the whole on a ground of two tones of the wall, lighter at the top, under its own grain. The icons are Carnet's own, Onde: one even ink line where every straight stroke takes one soft wave and frames swell a little, clean at 16px. Motion is short and settling; a pebble pressed gives rounder and springs back, sheets never change shape; the snip is the one authored moment. Both clients paint from the same data in `design/` (palette and its grounds, shapes, outline, pebbles, backgrounds, emblems, glyphs, collage, mark), so the phone (Android, primary) and the web twin cannot drift. The research behind the corner, the radius scale and keeping organic forms off text is in `docs/DESIGN-RESEARCH.md`.

Rejected by the direction contract and the owner (2026-10-22): per-item random outlines, wobbling or bowed edges, organic shapes holding text, ornamented chrome, Keep's plain rounded rectangles, the old metro's navy signage. Pinned by the owner the same evening: icons in one ink, original and gently wavy but clean (never coloured, never faces or kawaii), and a ground of two tones only (beige to a darker beige; grey to a darker grey at night), which stays Carnet's own ground: since 2026-10-27 a person may lay another on their device (Réglages > Fond), never one that breaks a ratio. Kept owner pins: green only as the matcha accent; icons and checkboxes neutral on any note colour; nothing behind an icon's line but a pebble tint; no shadow behind any icon, emblem or illustration; hovers and presses stay inside the control and never tilt or jump.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Sheets: one continuous corner (Figma's, smoothing 0.6) from a short radius scale by role, capped at 0.3 of the shorter side, nested concentrically, a hairline edge.
- One pebble: the mark's stone, stretched to its box and turned one of four ways, never under text; pressed, it gives toward round and springs back.
- Controls are soft sheets: their radius stays under half their height, never a capsule.
- Terracotta for filled actions and links; matcha for capture, the chosen, focus and switches.
- Earth pigments only on emblems, label tags, stones' washes and note washes; a note may also wear a soft mesh gradient, and a paper under either: three in faint ink (dots, grid, lines) and four in a pigment's ink (Seyès, millimetre, isometric dots, flecks).
- A hover or press is a tint laid inside the control's own shape, never a ripple past it, a tilt or a jump, and never touching another tint, a fill or an edge: tinted rows stand 4 apart (`GEOMETRY.rowGap`, the web's `--row-gap`), on both clients.
- Onde icons, Carnet's own: one even 1.9 line with round ends, a soft wave in every straight stroke, frames that swell a little, breathing circles; eleven glyphs take their solid form when their control is selected.
- Cut-outs hand-cut after Matisse: emblems and the label tag, flat, no cast shadow.
- A two-tone ground fixed to the window, lighter at the top, under a slightly stronger grain; quiet grain on every sheet, never stacked.
- System messages on a charcoal slip, dark in both modes.

## Colors

habits-garden's warm paper neutrals, a terracotta action family, a matcha accent family and fourteen earth pigments. Every value lives in `design/palette.ts`; its contrast test (`__tests__/palette.test.ts`) is the authority. Réglages calls the matcha family the primary colour ("Principale") and the terracotta family the accent ("Accent"): both can change per device, with the ground (see The Person's Colours and Ground, below). Pigment keys keep their old names because they sync (`yellow` is Safran, `grey` is Sauge); collections are still "lines" in code (`LineColor`, `LINES`, `--line-<key>`). Night values are the `night-*` tokens; the web switches them on `prefers-color-scheme` or `<html data-theme>`. The night ground is a warm black-grey from habits-garden's ink (OKLCH chroma under 0.01, warm hues 48 to 68), not its moss.

### Primary
- **Terracotta** (action, on-action): filled buttons, the one primary action on a surface.
- **Deep Terracotta** (link): links, text buttons, the "+ Collection" label, the reading track's scissors.
- **Peach** (highlight): text selection on both clients, and search matches (on the web with an underline in the text's own ink); at night a deep rust.

### Secondary
- **Deep Matcha** (focus): focus rings, carets and selection handles, the selected card ring, the chosen segment's tick, the selection bar's edge, the ring of a picker's chosen finish or emblem, switches.
- **Matcha Wash** (action-container, on-action-container): tonal buttons (kept matcha: a neutral tonal button read as disabled), the selection bar, a selected icon button's pebble, the chosen navigation place's pebble behind a glyph, the info card. A clear step darker than the paper by day. No pigment is ever laid on it.
- **Leaf Matcha** (leaf, on-leaf): the capture pebble, the selection tick pebble, a picker's check; never text.
- **Mark Matcha and Mark Beige** (mark-tile, mark-card): the mark's two colours, the same by day and night (`MARK_COLORS`): the pebble, and the beige shape what it holds is cut from (Carnet's card and its leaf, Pages' sheet and its writing, both with the card's uneven corners and its small turn); nowhere else. Another primary takes the pebble (`markColors`): itself when the card stands off it as far as on Carnet's own mark (3.4:1), else its hue deepened the least it must; the card stays beige.
- **Slip Matcha** (slip-action): "Annuler" and every action on the slip, and the slip's focus ring.

### Tertiary
- **The Pigments** (pigment-*, night lifts): Safran, Orange brûlé, Rouge, Framboise, Bordeaux, Lavande, Violet, Bleu, Sarcelle, Vert, Olive, Ocre, Brun, Sauge. A collection's emblem, a label's tag, a colour swatch's edge, the snip's cut once filed. The slip is dark in both themes, so a pigment drawn on it takes its night lift (the phone's `theme.slipLine`, the web's `[data-surface='slip']` tokens). Ink on a pigment is whichever of the two inks contrasts more (on-pigment-dark or on-pigment-light; night always night-on-pigment). Sauge belongs to "Non classé".
- **Washes** (wash-*): a coloured note's paper and a collection's stone (`--tint`, `theme.line[key].tint`), one OKLCH hue and chroma per pigment (`WASHES`), lightness 0.936 by day; at night lightness 0.345 (a step above the night card) with the chroma cut to 0.82 of the day's (at most 0.045). Ink holds 7:1 on every wash.
- **Text inks** (text-*, night-text-*; `--line-<key>-text`): a pigment as words on a page, the person's coloured text and code's colours, held at 4.5:1 on every paper, the well and every wash. A page's highlighted words lie on the pigment's wash.
- **Gradients** (`backgrounds.ts`): eight soft mesh washes a note can wear instead of a pigment (Aube, Lagon, Matcha latte, Abricot, Brume lavande, Nacre, Sous-bois, Terre cuite), pale by day, deep at night, ink 7:1 on every colour.

### Named Rules
**The Pigment Belongs To Cut-Outs Rule.** Pigments fill emblems, label tags and the snip's cut; a note and a stone wear the pigment's wash or a gradient, never the pigment itself. Chrome stays paper, terracotta and matcha. On a page, the person's own words may take a pigment's text ink or its wash, and code takes text inks: content, never chrome.

**The Terracotta Acts, Matcha Chooses Rule.** Terracotta fills and links; matcha marks capture, what is chosen, what has focus and what is switched on.

**The Green Is Only Matcha Rule.** Green appears only as matcha (the accent, and the sync dot's deep matcha): no neutral, night ground or veil carries a green cast, and icons and checkboxes stay neutral ink on any note colour. A person's primary takes matcha's place in every one of its roles and nowhere else.

**The Paper Under Pigment Rule.** A pigment never sits on the matcha fill. The chosen navigation place puts a collection's cut-out or a label's tag on its own wash pebble, a glyph on matcha; a picker marks a chosen cut-out with a paper pebble ringed in matcha.

**The Contrast Test Rule.** A new colour, wash or gradient enters `palette.ts`, `backgrounds.ts` or `appearance.ts` and passes its contrast test, or it does not ship (a person's own colours pass the same ratios at runtime, every palette, ground, random colour and generated gradient tested in `__tests__/appearance.test.ts`): ink 7:1 and the second ink 4.5:1 on every paper, wash and gradient; `edge` 3:1 on every ground; every ink, edge and pigment of the wall at its own ratio on both tones of the ground; a well 1.15 against the wall, the ground's top and 1.1 against a card; a disabled label 4.5:1 in its well; the sync dots 3:1 on the strip.

## Elevation & Depth

Paper off the ground: a 1px hairline edge plus a soft, warm two-layer lift by day and a single dark lift at night (`LIFTS` in `palette.ts`, the same CSS syntax on both clients). A container clipped to its outline on the web casts its lift from an unclipped wrapper as drop shadows (`--drop-*`, the blur halved), since a clip cuts a box-shadow off. The grain is the second depth cue: the ground's is fixed behind everything, each sheet carries its own between its colour and its content, never stacked on a nested surface.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`0 1px 1px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.05), 0 2px 6px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.05)`; night `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`): cards, chips, the search strip, the composer, the chosen segment.
- **Raised** (`0 2px 3px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.08), 0 6px 14px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.09)`; night `0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`): a card or the composer under the pointer.
- **Float** (`0 4px 10px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.12), 0 14px 30px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.14)`; night `0 6px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`): sheets, dialogs, menus, the drawer, the slip.
- **Bar lift** (the `shadow` colour, cast upwards: `0 -1px 3px` at 70% and `0 -6px 16px` at 60%): the web bottom bar alone.

### Named Rules
**The Shadow Moves, Text Never Does Rule.** Hover lifts a card from Card to Raised; selection is a ring laid over the sheet; content never shifts, and nothing tilts or jumps under the pointer.

**The Flat Cut-Out Rule.** Nothing cast or drawn on the paper has a shadow: emblems, stones, collage pieces, swatches, the tick pebble and the capture pebble lie flat. Lifts belong to sheets of paper.

**The Quiet Grain Rule.** Grain at 0.16 by day and 0.08 at night on sheets (`GRAIN` in `web/build/tokens.ts`; `grain` in the phone theme), tiled at 200 from a 256 procedural speckle; one layer per surface, never stacked, never on photos. The ground carries its own visible film grain on the web: every pixel of a 256 tile moves a little (`web/public/ground-grain.png`, strength baked into the file, both from `tools/textures/build_grain.py`), laid at its own size over the two tones as one more background layer (`--ground-paint`), so the header, the bars and the drawer that paint the ground paint it whole; the phone lays the same grain (`mobile/notes/assets/textures/ground-grain.png`, its pixels doubled, one to one on the screen's pixels, as the widgets do: `Grain ground`) over its ground and its drawer. Réglages turns each grain off on that device: the sheets' in Apparence ("Grain du papier": `html[data-grain='off']` on the web, the paper `Grain` drawing nothing on the phone), the ground's in Fond ("Grain sur le fond", the appearance's own `grain`: `html[data-ground-grain='off']`, `theme.groundGrain`); a surface without is flat colour. Home-screen widgets keep theirs (they follow the system, not the app).

## Shapes

Two forms, and only two: the sheet and the pebble (`design/shapes.ts`, `outline.ts`, `pebbles.ts`).

**Sheets.** Every container and control is a sheet with one continuous corner: Figma's construction at smoothing 0.6 (`SMOOTHING`, `squircleSegments`), a short circular arc between two easing curves, so the bend grows from nothing and no hump shows. One radius per role from a short scale (`RADII`, px on the web, dp on the phone): detail 5, small 9, chip 12, row 12, frame 12, control 13, bar 15, menu 18, card 20, panel 22, dialog 28; the segment is the bar less its 4 inset (11), and a shape nested in another is concentric with it (`concentric`: the parent's radius less the padding). No corner takes more than 0.3 of the box's shorter side (`CORNER_CAP`), so a one-line note stays a sheet, never a pill. The same radius serves every item of a role at every size: no per-item seed, no random corner, no bowed side. A sheet standing on a screen edge keeps those corners square: the bottom sheet (28 on its two top corners), a side sheet on the right edge (28 on its open side), the drawer (square, flush, full height). The info card is a panel whose corner by its pointer is tight (5), so the pointer stands on a straight edge (`bubble`).

Both clients draw the same corner. The web clips a container to its path (`useOutline`, `OutlinedBox`, or `data-outline="kind"` for a page's blocks: `card`, `panel`, `frame`, `menu`), draws its edge along it from inside (`OutlineEdge`) and its focus ring 4 out; a control takes `border-radius: var(--cut-*)`, made continuous with `corner-shape: squircle` where the browser has it, its radii scaled by `--corner-scale` (1.84 there, 1 elsewhere) so a browser without it (Firefox, the owner's) draws a round corner of the same depth. The phone draws it in its native view (`Organic`, Android's `OrganicView` with `Squircle.kt`, number for number), from `corners(SHAPES.role)` or `outline="kind"`; a plain `View` draws circles.

**The pebble.** The stone the mark's card lies on (`MARK_PEBBLE`), stretched to touch every side of its box, turned one of four ways (`PEBBLE_TURNS`): neighbours differ (`pebbleTurn(i)`), and a collection keeps its own stone for life (`pebbleTurnOf(id)`). Pressed, it gives 0.62 of the way toward the circle through its nodes and springs back: on the web `clip-path: shape(...)` in percentages (`--pebble-N`, `--pebble-N-pressed`; `data-pebble` clips the element, `data-pebble-under` paints it on `::before` so the focus ring stays whole, `data-still` keeps it from giving), in 90ms on `--ease-out` and back in 380ms on `--ease-spring`; on the phone `Organic` or `Tappable` with `pebble` (`pebbleNumbers`), the native press morph on `given`, 90ms decelerate in, 380ms spring back. Reduced motion holds it still. The pebble serves the capture pebble, an icon button's tint, a checkbox's tint, the chosen navigation place, a collection's stone, swatches and finish tiles, the selection tick, the open info and pictures glimpsed (a collection card's thumbnails). It never holds text. Dots (the sync dot, a picture's page dots, Agenda's event and busy dots, a calendar's colour, the « now » marker) are circles (`corner-shape: round` where the browser draws squircles).

**Cut-outs.** Hand-cut after Matisse, from data: irregular outlines on a 100 grid (`emblems.ts`, drafted by `tools/emblems/draft_emblems.py`). Fourteen emblems: feuille, étoile, soleil, fleur, trèfle, galet (even-odd), cœur, lune, goutte, nuage, vague, poisson (even-odd), oiseau, arche. "Non classé" always wears the tray ("bac"). A label wears a hand-cut paper tag (`LABEL_TAG_PATH` in a 96 by 68 box) drawn by both clients' `LabelMark`. Empty-state collages sit on a 240 by 150 board and are laid down as the view opens, piece by piece in drawing order (`collageEntrances`): each cut-out drifts in from just outside its place (10, from 0.92) and turns 12° into it, the paper sheet slides up 14 and settles from 7° steeper, 420 each on emphasized decelerate, 70 apart; Reduce Motion keeps the web's fades and shows the phone's still. The mark: a beige card turned -6°, a leaf and stem cut out of it in one even-odd path, on a matcha pebble (`mark.ts`, `MARK_COLORS`; in the apps the pebble takes the primary, `markColors`); the card path alone is the one-colour silhouette; every launcher, widget and web icon draws these paths.

**The Calm Wall Rule.** One continuous corner per role from `RADII`, the same for every item and every size, capped at 0.3 of the shorter side and concentric when nested. Never a seed, a random corner, a bowed side or a radius invented outside the scale.

**The Soft Sheet Control Rule.** A control's radius stays under half its height (a button 13 at 48, a field 13 at 52, the strip 15 at 54): it reads as a soft sheet, never a capsule, so Firefox without `corner-shape` draws what Chromium draws. The one exception is a switch: its track keeps the universal capsule and its thumb a circle, the shape everyone reads as a toggle.

**The Pebble Holds No Text Rule.** The pebble marks what is chosen, captured or kept, and crops glimpsed pictures; text always sits on a sheet. A link's own picture keeps its 4:5 frame, so products compare whole.

## Motion

Material 3's curves and one set of durations on both clients (`mobile/shared/src/ui/theme/motion.ts`; `--ease-*`, `--duration-*` in `web/shared/src/ui/styles/base.css`): 150 for feedback, 250 for a state change, 300 for something large arriving (a sheet, the drawer, the slip), never longer outside the snip, the pebble's spring back and an empty state's collage being laid down. Arrivals on emphasized decelerate, state changes on standard, departures on emphasized accelerate and shorter (150, or 200 for a sheet, the drawer and the slip). A press tint fades out in 150 once the finger lifts. Nothing grows from nothing: an arrival starts at 0.9 or more. Overlays grow from where they were opened, play their exit, and their host stays mounted while it runs.

The pebble's give is the signature interaction: 90 in, 380 back on a spring (`--ease-spring`, `cubic-bezier(0.42, 1.67, 0.21, 0.9)`; the phone's `PathInterpolator` with the same numbers). Sheets never change shape.

Gestures follow the finger 1:1 and hand its speed on: back to rest on M3's spatial spring (`spatial`, `spatialSlow` for a sheet or the drawer), or thrown off keeping the hand's speed (`throwTo`). A swipe that will act says so before it is let go: its threshold clicks (`mobile/notes/src/platform/haptics.ts`) and what it uncovers turns full strength; a long press that picks something up clicks too.

In lists, something leaving fades where it was, the rest close ranks (250, standard), something arriving settles in once they have made room. A wall replaced whole just goes; cards scrolled into the window never animate. Going deeper on the phone takes Android's own transition (`animation: 'default'`); a note opens with `fade_from_bottom`. A contact's card opens in three beats as the screen slides in (the face settling from 0.94, then the ways to reach them, then what is known, each rising 8 a beat later, 250); a face starred elsewhere settles into the favourites' row as the others make room. Reduce Motion: movement goes; the web keeps fades and colour changes, the phone cuts.

The web bottom bar's is 62 by 58; under the finger its tint fills the pebble and the pebble gives toward round, then springs back; on the web hover darkens it 8% toward its ink. In the web composer it is a flat 32 pebble that gives when the composer is pressed. Extended (with a label), it becomes a button-shaped sheet.

### Collection Chips, Stones and Emblems

Sizes: a chip 36 by 32 (emblem 21), a collection card 44 by 40 (26), a tile 64 by 58 (40, emblem turned -6°), its page's head 88 by 80 on the web (56) and 68 by 62 on the phone (44), the emblem turned -8°. ### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** the card radius (20), identical for every note; panels 22, dialogs 28, menus 18, frames 12.
- **Background:** cream paper, a wash, or a gradient, with an optional paper: dots 18, grid 20 and ruled lines aligned on the text in faint ink; Seyès (a Violet rule on each line of text, three faint Bleu rules between, squares a line wide), millimetre (5 squares in faint Orange brûlé, every fifth line stronger), isometric dots (Sarcelle, 18 apart, rows alternating half a step) and flecks (Rouge, Bleu, Ocre, Vert and Violet fibres on tiles of unrelated sizes, so no repeat shows) in a pigment's ink (`backgrounds.ts`); in the editor ruled papers (lines, Seyès: `isRuledPattern`) run the whole sheet, top to foot, in step with the text's lines and scrolling with it; a text note's title ends on the body's rules and one line is skipped before the text on every paper, and the ticked group of a checklist sits flush under the others (its hairline taking no room), so picking a paper never moves the words: the paper aligns on the text, never the other way. Collection cards stay cream.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Card at rest, Raised on hover (web), cast along the outline.
- **Border:** a 1px hairline along the outline, on both clients. Selection turns the edge into a 2.5px matcha ring over the sheet and pins a 26 leaf-matcha tick pebble half outside the top-right corner, ringed 2px in the ground: the ring fades in (150), the tick settles from half its size (250, decelerate); a long press clicks as it picks the card up.
- **Internal Padding:** 14; the 40 footer, only with labels or a reminder, carries outlined reminder chips (small radius, 24 high) and labels as a pigment tag plus their name in meta; on a card too narrow for both (`footerShowsLabelNames`), the tags alone. A collection card's thumbnails are pictures cropped to pebbles, wells until they load.
- **Height:** exactly the content's, computed from the card's anatomy (see Layout).
- **Checklists:** only written open items show (up to 6) and count toward the height.
- **Swipe:** a card swipes sideways to the archive, turning up to 3°, above every other card, growing see-through as it goes. Its threshold (38% of its width) clicks; let go past it or flung, it is thrown off with the hand's speed and the wall closes ranks; short of it, it springs back.
- **Web:** the select pebble shows under a hovering pointer, on keyboard focus and while picking; hidden, it takes no tap. On touch a hold (500) picks the card, with the press tint after 100.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** the field's veil (`fieldVeil`), control radius (13), a fixed metaStrong label above (never floating), the idle edge in `edge` (1px on the phone, 1.5px inset on the web), minimum height 52, 18 horizontal padding. A multiline field grows with what is typed (96 to 320 on the web), with no resize grip; on the phone its words stay still as a line is added (`useGrowingInput`: Android would scroll the new line into view a frame before the field grows, the first line jumping out of sight and back).
- **Focus:** the edge becomes 2px matcha; padding gives back the difference so text never moves; matcha caret and selection handles over peach selected text.
- **Error:** the 2px danger edge, and under the field the `error` glyph (16) and the sentence in danger ink, in place of its help: a problem says itself by its form as well as its colour.
- **What it holds** (`kind`, `design/fields.ts`, one table for both clients): its keyboard (digits, e-mail, telephone, address), its capitals (a name's words, a postal code's letters), whether it is corrected (never a name, an address or a code), and whether autofill may fill it (the person's own server address and token only, kept as a pair by a password manager; a contact's or an event's fields would be offered the person's own name and street, so they say no). Never the number input (its wheel and arrows change values by accident): digits come from the input mode. A hidden value (a key, a token) has its eye at the field's end (`eye`, `eyeOff`), built into the field.
- **Optional, counted:** « (facultatif) » in meta after the label, only where most fields must be filled (an S3 region); a form whose fields are mostly optional (a contact) marks none. Near its limit (from 80%), a field counts what it holds at the end of its note line (« 172 / 200 », tabular), in danger past it.
- **Choices as fields** (`SelectField` on the web, `PickerField` on the phone): the field's label, paper and edge, the value, a `chevronDown` at its end; the web opens the app's menu (the chosen value ticked), the phone a sheet of rows. Never the browser's own `<select>`. Two to four short choices are a segmented track instead.
- **Search strip:** field colour, bar radius (15), an outline edge, height 54; on the web frosted (the field at 74% over a 16px blur), the phone keeps it opaque. The add-link and new-label strips keep the same field look.
- **Switch:** the one capsule (the Soft Sheet Control Rule's exception), the same on both clients (`controls.module.css` `.switch`; the phone's `Switch` and `SwitchMark`, never Android's own): a 52 by 32 track edged 2px, its dot 16 off and 24 on, 2 in from the track. Off: the well edged in `edge`, the dot in the second ink. On: matcha (`focus`) fill and edge, the dot in on-action. The colours change in 150 and the dot travels in 250 (standard). A row whose words name the setting is the switch itself (`SwitchMark` at its end); a switch alone takes a 48 target. Off, the dot is in ink-faint.
- **Place field** (`PlaceField`, both clients): the places the device knows first (a `clock` glyph: a contact's address, an event's place used before), then the server's (a `mapPin`, Photon's, after a 250 to 300 pause, three letters at least), each its name in body over its detail in meta, an OpenStreetMap credit in label-small under them; nothing found says the place is kept as written. On the web a combobox in the menu's paper by the field (rows 48, the row radius). On the phone, as Google Calendar does it, the field (or the event form's place row) is a press that opens the place search over the whole screen (`PlaceSearch`, shown by the screen's `PlaceSearchHost`): the ground, the search strip at its top holding the caret (Back at its start, a × at its end), and the places under it with all the room above the keyboard, so nothing in the form moves while a place is looked for; with nothing typed, the places already used (and « Effacer le lieu » when it holds one); as words come, « Utiliser « … » » first (Enter does the same), then the places; a place picked, or the words kept, go back to the form, which Back leaves as it was. It rises 24 into place (250, decelerate) and fades away (150).
- **Mentions** (« @ »): one ranking everywhere (`core/services/mentionSearch`); on the web a menu by the caret, kinds under small heads; on the phone the rows ride on the keyboard in that same small sheet while « @word » is typed, a tap putting the mention in place, the keyboard staying.

### Forms
One way to fill something in, in every app and on both clients; research behind it: GOV.UK's validation pattern, Baymard's mobile keyboards, Smart Interface Design Patterns' inline validation, Material's full-screen dialog.
- **Labels stay:** each field's name sits above it for good (metaStrong), never a placeholder standing in for it; an example goes in its help, under it. An event's rows are the one exception, Google Calendar's: each led by its glyph in a 24 slot, the row naming itself to screen readers (its place a row too, « Ajouter un lieu » in link ink, opening the place search).
- **Say it once:** a word that repeats what the screen already shows goes, its words kept for screen readers: no title over fields whose own names say it (the contact form's identity fields, under the face that heads them: Prénom, Nom de famille, Entreprise, Fonction), no field name under a section that names it (« Anniversaire » needs no « Date »), and no word over a choice whose look says what it is (a colour's swatches, a collection's cut-outs, the gradient's colours, a reminder's day and hour chips) when a glyph or the title leads it. Help that restates its label goes too; what cannot be seen stays (a format, a cost, a consequence).
- **One column**, grouped under section titles; two short fields side by side only when read together (a postal code and its town, a day and its time). A field's width says how long its answer is (a postal code, a time).
- **When it says what is wrong** (`core/services/formChecks`, `useForm` on both clients): nothing while a fresh value is typed; leaving a field with something in it checks it; an empty field is said to be missing only once the form was sent; a field showing a problem checks again at every keystroke and goes quiet the moment it is right, while a field that was right and is edited waits until it is left. Sending shows every problem at once, takes the focus to the first (scrolled to the middle of the screen) and says how many by the actions (`FormMessage`, « 2 champs sont à corriger. », the `error` glyph in danger ink); TalkBack and screen readers hear it.
- **The primary action is never disabled to say why:** pressed with problems, it shows them. It sinks to the well only when there is nothing to act on (an empty question, no link to file) or while it runs (busy: it spins and refuses a second press). What the server refuses comes back under the field it concerns (a token, an address) with what was typed kept, else on the form's line, never as a slip alone.
- **Words name the act:** « Créer », « Ajouter », « S'abonner », « Enregistrer » for a change; a problem says what is wrong and how to fix it (« Une adresse commençant par https://, comme https://example.org/agenda.ics. »).
- **One way to save per form, never mixed:** a setting (Réglages, a switch, a segmented choice) applies at once, undoable by a slip when it matters; a value typed in a setting (a budget, a city) applies on « Appliquer », Enter or when left; a record's form (a contact, an event, a collection) saves on its button, ⌘/Ctrl+Enter or ⌘/Ctrl+S on the web.
- **Where the actions are:** a phone form that is its own screen is Material's full-screen dialog: × at the top bar's start (`closes`), the title, « Enregistrer » at its end, always in reach above the keyboard. A sheet keeps its actions pinned at its foot under a hairline (`FormFoot`: « Annuler » as text, the primary filled at the end), riding on the keyboard, never at the end of what scrolls. A web dialog keeps them in its still foot (`DialogFrame`); a web form that is its own page keeps them in a foot that sticks to the window's bottom while the form scrolls.
- **The keyboard moves on** (phone): each one-line field's action says « Suivant » and goes to the next field with the keyboard staying up; the last says « OK » and closes it, or sends a sheet of one or two fields. Taps on the form's buttons work at the first press with the keyboard up. On the web, Enter in a one-line field sends the form; Shift+Enter or a multiline field makes a new line; Escape closes a dialog.
- **Nothing typed is lost by a slip** (`useUnsaved`, both clients): while a form holds changes, leaving its page or screen (a link, the navigation, Back, the gesture, the tab closed) or closing its dialog or sheet (Escape, the scrim, a drag) asks « Abandonner les modifications ? » with « Continuer à modifier » and « Abandonner » (danger). A form that saves as it goes (a note, a link's note) never asks.
- **A date typed:** one field on the digit keyboard (a birthday), its separators written as it is typed (`core/services/dateTyping`): a part's two digits, or one that cannot start two (a day from 4, a month from 2), bring its « / »; a separator typed after one digit pads it; a backspace over a separator takes the digit before it; a paste is laid out; an edit inside stays as typed and settles when left. Its format stays in its help (« JJ/MM/AAAA, année facultative », the date read after a dot), and examples take a day past 12 (27/03).
- **Opening:** a form made to type into (a new label, a link to file, a question) puts the caret in its first field; a record's form (a contact, an event) opens on the record with the keyboard down, except a new one, whose first field takes it.

On a board card, faces alone at the foot.
- **An address:** a card whose frame shows a small plan drawn from the address itself (streets, maybe water and a park, a pin; nothing fetched, the same address always the same plan), the street in body strong, the town and country in body, its kind in meta; a long press copies it. In the week before a birthday, a contact's « À propos » (phone) holds « Prévenir la veille » over the rule it follows. A contact's row in the list (phone) slides as a link row does: right to call (`phoneCall`), left to text (`bubble`), on the primary's container colour, its threshold (30%) clicking, acting only when let go past it, never on a throw; Reduce Motion keeps the row still and fades the action in.

The page stays the loudest thing.

Corbeille, Réglages and Administration close both lists. - **Web, one app per web app:** each app is a web app of its own (`/notes`, `/pages`), its navigation its own (Carnet's board, collections and labels; Pages' tree); there is no switch between apps in the navigation. An app announced but not ready appears only in « Applications » and the setup, quiet, its glyph on a grey pebble, with « Bientôt disponible ».

### Overlays
- **Drawer:** the ground itself (its two tones, its grain) against the screen's left edge, its full height (under the status bar, over the gesture bar), square, flush, its open side a straight edge in the outline colour, Float lift, grained, 312 wide (86% at most); on the web under 1100px the same square sheet (`Layer` kind `start`, no outline). ### The Slip (snackbar)
System messages on the charcoal slip (dark in both modes): bar radius (15), Float lift, body text in on-slip; the action is its own chip (40 high, chip radius) edged in cream at 22%, its label in slip matcha. Each slip says its tone with a leading glyph, 16 in from its left edge and 12 before the text: info (`info`, on-slip-muted), success (`check`, slip matcha), warning (`alert`, the night Safran) and error (`error`, the night danger), each 4.5:1 or more on the charcoal; the right edge keeps the same inset (the action chip 6 in, as from its top and foot). The snip keeps its own scissors and no glyph. It rises a short way into place (250, decelerate) and sinks away in 150; swiped sideways it is thrown off. It leaves after 4s, or 10s with an action; one saying Claude is at work has no action and stays until its answer replaces it (two minutes at most). No close button. A success's tick is in Slip Matcha (the primary's); a message is never cut.

### The Bin
Every app's bin, on both clients, as Carnet's: its title, then once in meta how long things stay (« … sont supprimés au bout de {days} jours. ») while it holds something; « Vider la corbeille » with the `broom` at its head (a text button by the title, or an icon button in a phone's top bar), asking « Vider la corbeille ? » with « Vider » (danger), its slip counting what went; each row restores (a success slip with « Annuler ») or deletes for good (`trash`, asked first: « Supprimer définitivement ? », « Supprimer », then a success slip with no « Annuler »). Empty, it is the `trash` collage, « La corbeille est vide » and « … y restent {days} jours, le temps de changer d’avis. ». A record in the bin says so on its own page in a panel with « Restaurer ». In Réglages › Données, « Vider la corbeille » (`trash`) says how many things it holds, or « La corbeille est vide. », with nothing to press while empty.

### The Administration

### Icons

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every radius from `RADII` by role (`SHAPES`, `OUTLINES`; `--cut-*` on the web, `corners(SHAPES.role)` or `outline="kind"` on an `Organic` or `Tappable` on the phone), concentric when nested.
- **Do** keep a control's radius under half its height, so it reads as a soft sheet in every browser.
- **Do** use the pebble for what is chosen, captured or kept and for glimpsed pictures (`data-pebble`, `data-pebble-under`, `pebble` on the phone), each row's neighbours on different turns and a collection's stone on `pebbleTurnOf(id)`.
- **Do** let a pressed pebble give and spring back (90 in, 380 back); keep sheets still.
- **Do** take every colour from `palette.ts` or `backgrounds.ts` through the theme or `var(--…)`, and pass its contrast test.
- **Do** edge cards, chips and bars with the 1px hairline; menus, dialogs, sheet lips and field-like strips with the outline; a control drawn by its edge alone with `edge`.
- **Do** fill actions in terracotta, write links in deep terracotta, and mark capture, the chosen, focus and switches in matcha; keep tonal buttons matcha wash.
- **Do** hover and press inside the control's own shape: 6% of the control's ink under a hovering pointer and 10% under the press (a link-coloured control: 8% and 12%).
- **Do** put grain on the ground and on every sheet at the theme's strength, one layer per surface.
- **Do** lift paper with the shared lifts only: Card at rest, Raised for hover, Float for sheets, dialogs, menus, the drawer and the slip.
- **Do** size a board card from its content with `layoutCard` (`wall.ts`), never by measuring it.
- **Do** keep a link's own picture in its 4:5 frame.
- **Do** settle arrivals on decelerate within 150, 250 or 300ms, and send departures off faster on accelerate.
- **Do** keep Signika alone, set by family on the phone, with the night grade after dark.
- **Do** lay a screen on the ground (`Ground` on the phone, `var(--ground-paint)` on the web), and draw any new icon in Onde (`tools/icons/onde.mjs`).
- **Do** take the primary's and the accent's roles and the ground from the appearance resolved for the theme (`theme.color` and `theme.ground` on the phone, the custom properties on the web); a pinned bar shows the ground's slice (`GroundSlice`), never a flat tone of it.

### Don't:
- **Don't** give an item its own random corners, a seeded outline or bowed sides; every item of a role takes the same radius.
- **Don't** round a control into a capsule or a container past `CORNER_CAP`.
- **Don't** put text on a pebble, or crop a link's own picture to one.
- **Don't** put a stone in a navigation row other than the chosen place's (on the phone, none: the chosen row lies on its wash).
- **Don't** float or round the drawer; it runs the full height, square and flush.
- **Don't** paint chrome, text or a whole note in a pigment; notes and stones wear a wash.
- **Don't** use matcha for a filled action or terracotta for selection or focus.
- **Don't** lay a pigment on the matcha fill: a chosen cut-out sits on its own wash or on paper.
- **Don't** bounce anything but the snip's landing and the pebble's spring back.
- **Don't** grow anything from nothing: arrivals start at 0.9 or more.
- **Don't** unmount an overlay before its exit has played.
- **Don't** set uppercase anywhere but the stamp.
- **Don't** draw anything behind an icon's line but its control's pebble tint.
- **Don't** fade a disabled button's pigment or opacity; it sinks to a well.
- **Don't** cast a shadow behind an icon, emblem, stone, swatch, pebble or illustration.
- **Don't** tilt, lift, translate or grow a control under the pointer (static turns such as the stamp, a stone's emblem and the ghost emblem are drawn, not hovers).
- **Don't** stack grain on a nested surface.
- **Don't** colour an icon, give it a face, or bring in an icon library; don't add a third tone to Carnet's own ground, and don't let a colour a person picks reach the screen unfitted.
- **Don't** hard-code a hex, a radius or a shadow in a component; the web reads generated custom properties, the phone reads the theme.
