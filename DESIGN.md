# Design System: Carnet

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Galet et feuille: calm sheets resting on pebbles"**

One system for every app of the suite (Carnet, Pages, Contacts, Agenda, Clock), named after the first and drawn after its mark: a beige card on a matcha pebble. The phone (Android) is the primary client, the web its twin; both paint from the same data in `design/` (`packages/design/src`), so they cannot drift. The research behind the corner, the radius scale and keeping organic forms off text is in `docs/DESIGN-RESEARCH.md`.

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Sheets:** every container and control has one continuous corner, the same for every item of a role at every size, so a hundred notes read as one quiet wall.
- **One pebble:** the only organic form; it marks what is chosen, captured or kept and never holds text.
- **Terracotta acts, matcha chooses;** earth pigments only on cut-outs and washes. A person may pick other colours and another ground per device, always fitted to the system's ratios.
- **Keep's density:** each card exactly as tall as its content.
- **One face,** Signika, at fixed sizes; weight carries rank.
- **Paper on paper:** a hairline edge, one soft lift, quiet grain, on a two-tone ground fixed to the window.
- **Feedback is a tint inside the control's own shape:** never a ripple past it, a tilt or a jump, never touching another tint, a fill or an edge.
- **Short, settling motion;** the pebble's give and the snip are the only authored moments.
- **System messages** on a charcoal slip, dark in both themes.

## Colors

Warm paper neutrals, a terracotta action family, a matcha accent family and fourteen earth pigments.

- Every value lives in `design/palette.ts`; its contrast test (`design/__tests__/palette.test.ts`) is the authority.
- Réglages calls the matcha family the primary ("Principale") and the terracotta family the accent ("Accent"); both can change per device, with the ground.
- Pigment keys keep the names they sync under (`yellow` is Safran, `grey` is Sauge); collections are "lines" in code (`LineColor`, `LINES`, `--line-<key>`).
- Night values are the `night-*` tokens; the web switches on `prefers-color-scheme` or `<html data-theme>`. The night ground is a warm black-grey (OKLCH chroma under 0.01, hues 48 to 68), never a green one.

### Primary
- **Terracotta** (action, on-action): filled buttons, the one primary action on a surface.
- **Deep Terracotta** (link): links, text buttons, the "+ Collection" label, the reading track's scissors.
- **Peach** (highlight): text selection, and search matches (the web adds an underline in the text's own ink); at night a deep rust.

### Secondary
Matcha is the primary's family: wherever this document says matcha, matcha wash or leaf, a device that chose another primary shows that colour's same role (« the primary's wash » is Matcha Wash, « the focus edge » Deep Matcha).
- **Deep Matcha** (focus): focus rings, carets and selection handles, the selected card ring, the chosen segment's tick, the selection bar's edge, the ring of a picker's chosen finish or emblem, switches.
- **Matcha Wash** (action-container, on-action-container): tonal buttons (a neutral one reads as disabled), the selection bar, a selected icon button's pebble, the chosen navigation place's pebble behind a glyph, the info card. A clear step darker than the paper by day.
- **Leaf Matcha** (leaf, on-leaf): the capture pebble, the selection tick pebble, a picker's check; never text.
- **Mark Matcha and Mark Beige** (mark-tile, mark-card; `MARK_COLORS`): the mark's pebble and its beige card, the same by day and night, nowhere else.
- **Slip Matcha** (slip-action): "Annuler" and every action on the slip, and the slip's focus ring.

### Tertiary
- **The Pigments** (pigment-*, night lifts): Safran, Orange brûlé, Rouge, Framboise, Bordeaux, Lavande, Violet, Bleu, Sarcelle, Vert, Olive, Ocre, Brun, Sauge (which belongs to "Non classé"). A collection's emblem, a label's tag, a colour swatch's edge, the snip's cut once filed.
  - Ink on a pigment is whichever of the two inks contrasts more (on-pigment-dark or on-pigment-light; night always night-on-pigment).
  - On the slip, dark in both themes, a pigment takes its night lift (`theme.slipLine`; the web's `[data-surface='slip']` tokens).
- **Washes** (wash-*; `WASHES`, `--tint`, `theme.line[key].tint`): a coloured note's paper and a collection's stone. One OKLCH hue and chroma per pigment, lightness 0.936 by day; at night 0.345 (a step above the night card), chroma cut to 0.82 of the day's (at most 0.045). Ink holds 7:1 on every wash.
- **Text inks** (text-*, night-text-*; `--line-<key>-text`): a pigment as words on a page (coloured text, code), 4.5:1 on every paper, the well and every wash. Highlighted words lie on the pigment's wash.
- **Gradients** (`design/backgrounds.ts`): eight soft mesh washes a note can wear instead of a pigment (Aube, Lagon, Matcha latte, Abricot, Brume lavande, Nacre, Sous-bois, Terre cuite), pale by day, deep at night, ink 7:1 on every colour.

### The Person's Colours and Ground
Per device, never synced (`Appearance`, `design/appearance.ts`), resolved for each theme by `resolveAppearance`. The phone reads the result through `theme.color` and `theme.ground`; the web as custom properties laid on `<html>` through the CSSOM (`web/shared/src/ui/appearanceStyle.ts`; the page's policy refuses inline stylesheets). Their pickers are under Components › Colours and Ground.

- **Roles:** the primary is leaf, on-leaf, focus, action-container, on-action-container and slip-action; the accent is action, on-action, link and highlight. Signals stay theirs: ok, waiting, danger, the stamp, the mark's card.
- **Palettes** (`PALETTES`): eight pairs (Carnet's matcha and terracotta, Sarcelle, Myrtille, Glycine, Pivoine, Mimosa, Ardoise, Mousse), or each colour picked precisely.
- **A picked colour** is the capture pebble's (primary) or a filled button's (accent) as it is when an ink holds 4.5:1 on it (the accent also 3:1 off every surface). Every other role takes its hue at the depth the palette gives matcha or terracotta, moved in lightness the least it must to read where it is drawn, on the chosen ground too. Carnet's pair gives back the palette's own values.
- **The mark's pebble follows the primary** (`markColors`): itself when the card stands 3.4:1 off it (as on Carnet's own mark), else its hue deepened the least it must; the card stays beige. In the apps and the web's tab; Android's launcher shows the icon of the palette nearest the primary (`launcherPalette`, one launcher entry per palette, switched once the app has left the screen). Widgets, the icon in Android's settings and share sheet, and the web's install icons keep Carnet's matcha.
- **Grounds:** Carnet's two tones (the default); seven other gradients (two tones: Aube, Lagon, Brume, Sauge; clouds: Nacre, Opale, Sorbet); eight flat colours (Crème, Lin, Pierre, Nuage, Menthe, Ciel, Lilas, Rose); a colour of one's own; or a generated gradient of two to four colours, blended top to foot through OKLab (`fondu`, no grey dip between hues) or pooled as soft clouds on seeded anchors (`nuees`), laid out again by a new seed.
- **Every ground is fitted** (`fitGround`, `gradientPaint`), by day and at night: every ink, edge, default action and pigment of the wall keeps its ratio on it, and at night a card still stands 1.15:1 off it. A picked colour that fails is moved in lightness (pale by day, deep at night); a generated one keeps a margin so the browser's blends never cross the line.
- The ground's grain is a switch of its own ("Grain sur le fond").

### Neutral
- **The Ground** (ground-top, ground-bottom; `GROUNDS`, `groundGradient`): what every screen lies on, two tones from the window's top to its foot, fixed while content scrolls, under its own grain. By day cream to a warmer sand (#F4F0E2 to #E8D9B5, as deep as the pigments allow: Sarcelle, Safran and Sauge keep 3:1 at the foot); at night grey to near-black (#292725 to #11100F). The drawer paints it whole; a pinned bar (the header, a top bar, the bottom bar) paints the slice it covers (`GroundSlice`), so it reads as the ground and still hides what scrolls under it; the browser chrome takes its top.
- **Paper Ground** (wall): the ground's flat colour: sheets, dialogs, side sheets, the fallback under the gradient.
- **Cream Card** (paper): cards, chips, menus, the chosen segment, the paper pebble under a chosen cut-out. At night a card stands 1.15:1 off the ground's top and 1.5:1 off its foot.
- **Field Cream** (field): a step lighter than a card by day; chips and swatches drawn on the field's paper.
- **Field Veil** (fieldVeil, `FIELD_VEIL`): text fields, pickers drawn as fields, the search strips. The lightest cream laid over whatever they lie on (55% by day, 6% at night): Field Cream on a card, a lighter patch of a coloured ground, never a grey square. Ink, hint, edge and focus hold their ratios on it over every ground (`fieldOn`). The web's header search lays it over the ground's top, frosted.
- **Well** (paper-high): link thumbnails and image frames, link facts, a photo still loading, the trash banner, a disabled button, a switch's off track, the Keep drop zone.
- **Charcoal Slip** (slip, slip-high, on-slip, on-slip-muted): the snackbar and the snip panel.
- **Warm Ink** (ink, ink-muted, ink-faint): text and icons. Muted and faint are the same second ink, also an open checkbox's edge, a done item's filled box and a disabled button's label.
- **Control Edge** (edge): a control drawn by its edge alone (text fields, the empty swatch, a switch that is off, the web scrollbar thumb, the Keep drop zone). Solid: 3:1 on every ground.
- **Ink Veil** (veil): the neutral selected fill for a toggle on a note's own colour (the editor's pin, reminder and other pressed controls), where matcha would clash with the wash.
- **Ink Veils** (warm ink at low alpha):
  - hairline: the edge of cards, chips and bars;
  - outline: the firmer edge of menus, dialogs, the drawer's open side, sheet lips, the search, add-link and new-label strips, a link's image frame and footer chips; also the sheet handle and reading dashes;
  - track: sinks the segmented control;
  - dashed: the "+ Collection".
- **Signals:** danger, on-danger; ok (deep matcha) and waiting (Safran) for the sync dot, which breathes down to 0.6 while it syncs on the web; scrim (warm ink, 32% by day, 56% at night) under sheets, the drawer and every phone overlay; scrim-soft (20% and 48%) under a centred web dialog, over its blur; viewer behind a full photo; shadow for the web bottom bar's upward lift.

### Named Rules
**The Pigment Belongs To Cut-Outs Rule.** Pigments fill emblems, label tags and the snip's cut; a note and a stone wear the pigment's wash or a gradient, never the pigment itself. Chrome stays paper, terracotta and matcha. On a page, a person's own words may take a pigment's text ink or its wash, and code takes text inks: content, never chrome.

**The Terracotta Acts, Matcha Chooses Rule.** Terracotta fills and links; matcha marks capture, what is chosen, what has focus and what is switched on.

**The Green Is Only Matcha Rule.** Green appears only as matcha (the accent, and the sync dot's deep matcha): no neutral, night ground or veil carries a green cast, and icons and checkboxes stay neutral ink on any note colour. A person's primary takes matcha's place in every one of its roles and nowhere else.

**The Paper Under Pigment Rule.** A pigment never sits on the matcha fill. The chosen navigation place puts a collection's cut-out or a label's tag on its own wash pebble, a glyph on matcha; a picker marks a chosen cut-out with a paper pebble ringed in matcha.

**The Contrast Test Rule.** A new colour, wash or gradient passes its contrast test (`palette.ts`, `backgrounds.ts`, `appearance.ts`) or does not ship; a person's own colours pass the same ratios at runtime (`design/__tests__/appearance.test.ts`):
- ink 7:1 and the second ink 4.5:1 on every paper, wash and gradient;
- `edge` 3:1 on every ground;
- every ink, edge and pigment of the wall at its own ratio on both tones of the ground;
- a well 1.15 against the wall and the ground's top, 1.1 against a card;
- a disabled label 4.5:1 in its well;
- the sync dots 3:1 on the strip.

## Layout

**The wall.** A masonry wall with no grid: each card is exactly as tall as its content and goes to the shortest column.
- Heights are computed, never measured, from each card's anatomy and Signika's advance widths (`layoutCard` in `notes/services/wall.ts`; `design/advances.ts`, `design/textWrap.ts`), so both clients size a card the same way.
- Gaps 8, card padding 14. A title stops at 3 lines, a checklist at 6 rows, a text body at 16 lines and at a card height of 384 (328 in one column). The 40 footer appears only with labels or a reminder.
- A collection card (`COLLECTION_CARD`): its emblem (26) on its stone (44 by 40), the name 10 beside it on up to two lines, then its pictures and count (« Déjà faits · n » once every link is done).
- Two columns on the phone; on the web as many as fit (minimum 160px under 600px wide, 232px above). Cards sit 8 apart on the phone and under 720px, 12 apart wider.

**Targets and rows.**
- Touch targets are 48 minimum (`GEOMETRY.hit`).
- Rows whose press, hover or chosen state is a tint (navigation, menus, lists, a dialog's or a sheet's rows) stand 4 apart (`GEOMETRY.rowGap`, the web's `--row-gap`); a row on a card sits 6 in from its edges.
- On the phone a list row takes half the gap above and below (`ListRow`); a list laid out by number counts it (`LIST_ROW_PITCH`).

**First viewport (both clients).**
- A field-coloured search strip (menu, layout toggle, sync dot), 54 high, 8 below the inset: the only chrome pinned at the top. The matcha selection bar replaces it in place.
- Under it, scrolling with the wall: the collection chips, ending in a dashed "+ Collection"; then "Épinglées" and "Autres".
- The phone has no bar at its foot: the capture pebble floats over the wall, and the snackbar clears it.
- From 720px the web's board opens on a composer strip ("Créer une note…"), a small capture pebble at its left. From 1100px the docked navigation lists the collections, so the board shows no chips.

**One frame for every screen (web).**
- Every screen starts the same distance under the header, on the search strip's left edge (`--screen-top` 8, `--screen-side` 24; 4 and 12 under 720px), its title in one 56 head (`.viewHead`, `PageHead`), so a title lands in the same place everywhere.
- Carnet's and Pages' homes open alike: "Créer une note…" and "Créer une page…" are one strip (`ComposerStrip`, its key at its end: C, P), their section heads the wall's 40 band; under 720px both take the search header and the bottom bar (the pages' pebble makes a page).
- An empty place (`EmptyState`) is centred on the reading column under the search strip (760), so it never moves between screens.
- While a layer is open the page holds still and the scrollbar's room becomes padding (`html[data-locked]`): nothing shifts and the scrim reaches the window's edge.

**Web breakpoints.** Header row 64; the search strip caps at 760px (height 50).

**Page widths (web).** Two, in every app (`--page-narrow` 760 and `--page-wide` 1120, `base.css`), so a page's right edge never wanders: narrow for what is read or filled in (Réglages, a contact, a form, Clock's alarms, Agenda's day), wide for what is laid out as many (a collection in every layout, the collections, the contacts, a calendar, Pages' home, Wallet's cards, a collection's facts). A page's head takes its body's width: its actions end where the body ends. Pages' document column (below) is its own, centred.
- **Under 720px,** the phone layout: the search header heads the homes alone; every other page takes the phone's top bar (the ground, a back button, the title, its one action), pinned while the page scrolls; a page's title fades into the bar (150) only once its own has gone up under it. Carnet's bottom bar shows on the notes, Rappels and a label; it slides away while notes are picked (150, accelerate) and back (250, decelerate). Another page comes in whole with a 24px shift and a fade (250, decelerate), from the left going back.
- **Under 900px** the header's action ("Classer un lien", "Nouvelle page") folds to its icon.
- **From 1100px** a docked navigation column (264px) lists the app's places.

**A page (web).** A document column 760 wide with a 56 gutter on each side for the block handles. Under 720px the gutter is 30 on the left under a mouse; under a finger there is none (16 on each side) and the page keeps 96 at its foot for the block bar. Side-by-side columns stack; a table wider than the column scrolls sideways, its hidden right side fading and, under 720px, its first column staying in view.

## Elevation & Depth

Paper off the ground: a 1px hairline edge plus a soft, warm two-layer lift by day and a single dark lift at night (`LIFTS` in `palette.ts`, the same CSS syntax on both clients); grain is the second depth cue. A web container clipped to its outline casts its lift from an unclipped wrapper as drop shadows (`--drop-*`, the blur halved), since a clip cuts a box-shadow off.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`0 1px 1px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.05), 0 2px 6px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.05)`; night `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`): cards, chips, the search strip, the composer, the chosen segment.
- **Raised** (`0 2px 3px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.08), 0 6px 14px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.09)`; night `0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`): a card or the composer under the pointer.
- **Float** (`0 4px 10px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.12), 0 14px 30px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.14)`; night `0 6px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`): sheets, dialogs, menus, the drawer, the slip.
- **Bar lift** (the `shadow` colour, cast upwards: `0 -1px 3px` at 70% and `0 -6px 16px` at 60%): the web bottom bar alone.

### Named Rules
**The Shadow Moves, Text Never Does Rule.** Hover lifts a card from Card to Raised; selection is a ring laid over the sheet; content never shifts, and nothing tilts or jumps under the pointer.

**The Flat Cut-Out Rule.** Nothing cast or drawn on the paper has a shadow: emblems, stones, collage pieces, swatches, the tick pebble and the capture pebble lie flat. Lifts belong to sheets of paper.

**The Quiet Grain Rule.** One layer of grain per surface, between its colour and its content: never stacked on a nested surface, never on photos.
- Sheets: 0.16 by day, 0.08 at night (`GRAIN` in `web/build/tokens.ts`; `grain` in the phone theme), tiled at 200 from a 256 procedural speckle.
- The ground: its own, more visible film grain, fixed behind everything (`ground-grain.png` in `web/public` and `mobile/shared/assets/textures`, every pixel of a 256 tile moved a little, its strength in the file; `tools/textures/build_grain.py`). On the web it is a layer of `--ground-paint`, so whatever paints the ground (the header, the bars, the drawer) paints it whole; the phone lays it one to one on the screen's pixels (its pixels doubled) over its ground and its drawer.
- Réglages turns each off per device: "Grain du papier" (`html[data-grain='off']`; the phone's `Grain` draws nothing) and "Grain sur le fond" (the appearance's `grain`: `html[data-ground-grain='off']`, `theme.groundGrain`). Home-screen widgets keep theirs: they follow the system, not the app.

## Shapes

Two forms, and only two: the sheet and the pebble (`design/shapes.ts`, `outline.ts`, `pebbles.ts`).

### Sheets
- **The corner:** Figma's continuous corner at smoothing 0.6 (`SMOOTHING`, `squircleSegments`): a short circular arc between two easing curves, so the bend grows from nothing and no hump shows.
- **The scale** (`RADII`; px on the web, dp on the phone), one radius per role: detail 5, small 9, chip 12, row 12, frame 12, control 13, bar 15, menu 18, card 20, panel 22, dialog 28; the segment is the bar less its 4 inset (11).
- **Nested shapes** are concentric (`concentric`: the parent's radius less the padding).
- **In a container's corner,** whatever lies there (a row, a button's tint, a focus ring, a picker's choice) stands as far in from both sides (6 for rows, `ROW_INSET`) and takes the container's radius as drawn at its size, less that inset, on that corner only; its other corners keep their role, and one set further in than the curve allows keeps its own. The web hands it down as `--row-outer` from every measured outline (`cornerVars`; a popover of rows sits 6 in from every edge, as a menu); the phone as `PanelRowCorner` from a `RowPanel` (a settings `Section`).
- **On a screen edge** a sheet's corners there are square: the bottom sheet (28 on its two top corners), a side sheet (28 on its open side), the drawer (square, flush, full height).
- **The info card** is a panel whose corner by its pointer is tight (5), so the pointer stands on a straight edge (`bubble`).
- **Web:** a container is clipped to its path (`useOutline`, `OutlinedBox`, or `data-outline="kind"` for a page's blocks: `card`, `panel`, `frame`, `menu`), its edge drawn along it from inside (`OutlineEdge`), its focus ring 4 out. A control takes `border-radius: var(--cut-*)` with `corner-shape: squircle` where the browser has it, its radii scaled by `--corner-scale` (1.84 there, 1 elsewhere), so a browser without it (Firefox) draws a round corner of the same depth; a short control that cannot hold the longer corner is drawn round at the same depth.
- **Phone:** the native view draws it (`Organic`, Android's `OrganicView` with `Squircle.kt`, number for number), from `corners(SHAPES.role)` or `outline="kind"`. A plain `View` draws circles.

### The pebble
- The stone the mark's card lies on (`MARK_PEBBLE`), stretched to touch every side of its box, turned one of four ways (`PEBBLE_TURNS`): neighbours differ (`pebbleTurn(i)`), and a collection keeps its own stone for life (`pebbleTurnOf(id)`).
- It serves the capture pebble, an icon button's and a checkbox's tint, the chosen navigation place, a collection's stone, swatches and finish tiles, the selection tick, the open info, and pictures glimpsed (a collection card's thumbnails).
- Pressed, it gives 0.62 of the way toward the circle through its nodes (90ms) and springs back (380ms); reduced motion holds it still.
  - Web: `clip-path: shape(...)` in percentages (`--pebble-N`, `--pebble-N-pressed`), in on `--ease-out`, back on `--ease-spring`. `data-pebble` clips the element; `data-pebble-under` paints it on `::before` so the focus ring stays whole; `data-still` keeps it from giving.
  - Phone: `pebble` on an `Organic` or `Tappable` (`pebbleNumbers`), the native press morph on `given`, decelerate in, spring back.
- Dots are circles, not pebbles (the sync dot, a picture's page dots, Agenda's event and busy dots, a calendar's colour, the « now » marker): `corner-shape: round` where the browser draws squircles.

### Cut-outs
- Hand-cut after Matisse, from data: irregular outlines on a 100 grid (`design/emblems.ts`, drafted by `tools/emblems/draft_emblems.py`).
- Fourteen emblems: feuille, étoile, soleil, fleur, trèfle, galet (even-odd), cœur, lune, goutte, nuage, vague, poisson (even-odd), oiseau, arche. "Non classé" always wears the tray ("bac").
- A label wears a hand-cut paper tag (`LABEL_TAG_PATH`, in a 96 by 68 box), drawn by both clients' `LabelMark`.
- Empty-state collages sit on a 240 by 150 board (`design/collage.ts`; laid down as under Motion).
- The mark (`design/mark.ts`): a beige card turned -6°, the app's subject cut out of it in one even-odd path (Carnet's leaf and stem), on its pebble. The card path alone is the one-colour silhouette. Every launcher, widget and web icon draws these paths.

### Named Rules
**The Calm Wall Rule.** One continuous corner per role from `RADII`, the same for every item and every size, concentric when nested, and never more than 0.3 of the box's shorter side (`CORNER_CAP`): a one-line note stays a sheet, never a pill. Never a seed, a random corner, a bowed side or a radius invented outside the scale.

**The Soft Sheet Control Rule.** A control's radius stays under half its height (a button 13 at 48, a field 13 at 52, the strip 15 at 54): a soft sheet, never a capsule, so a browser without `corner-shape` draws what Chromium draws. The one exception is a switch: a capsule track and a round thumb, the shape everyone reads as a toggle.

**The Pebble Holds No Text Rule.** The pebble marks what is chosen, captured or kept, and crops glimpsed pictures; text always sits on a sheet. A link's own picture keeps its 4:5 frame, so products compare whole.

## Motion

Material 3's curves and one set of durations on both clients (`mobile/shared/src/ui/theme/motion.ts`; `--ease-*`, `--duration-*` in `web/shared/src/ui/styles/base.css`).

- **Durations:** 150 for feedback, 250 for a state change, 300 for something large arriving (a sheet, the drawer, the slip). Never longer, but for the snip, the pebble's spring back, an empty state's collage and a collection's ghost being laid down.
- **Curves:** arrivals on emphasized decelerate; state changes on standard; departures on emphasized accelerate and shorter (150, or 200 for a sheet, the drawer and the slip). A press tint fades out in 150 once the finger lifts.
- **Nothing grows from nothing:** an arrival starts at 0.9 or more.
- **Overlays** grow from where they were opened and play their exit, their host staying mounted while it runs.
- **The pebble's give** is the signature interaction: 90 in, 380 back on a spring (`--ease-spring`, `cubic-bezier(0.42, 1.67, 0.21, 0.9)`; the phone's `PathInterpolator` with the same numbers). Sheets never change shape.
- **Reduce Motion:** movement goes; the web keeps fades and colour changes, the phone cuts.

**Gestures (phone).**
- They follow the finger 1:1 and hand its speed on: back to rest on M3's spatial spring (`spatial`; `spatialSlow` for a sheet or the drawer), or thrown off keeping the hand's speed (`throwTo`).
- A swipe that will act says so before it is let go: its threshold clicks (`mobile/shared/src/platform/haptics.ts`) and what it uncovers turns full strength. A long press that picks something up clicks too.
- A row that acts in place (a link stamped, a contact called or texted) acts only when let go past 30% of its width (`SWIPE_SHARE`), never on a throw.
- Something that goes away (a card archived, a period turned, a sheet, the drawer, a photo) goes past its threshold or when flung (`FLING_SPEED`, 1000); a fling clicks as it is let go. The drawer's threshold is 35%.
- Every list a person pulls down syncs (`usePullToSync`: each app's home, a collection, Agenda's views but the month).

**Lists.** Something leaving fades where it was, the rest close ranks (250, standard), something arriving settles in once they have made room, in every app's lists, bins and labels.
- Phone: `listMove`; `listIn` from 0.94 in 200 after 120; `listInAtOnce` for an item just typed; `listOut` in 150 on accelerate; `useArrivals`. Web: `web/shared/src/ui/useListMotion.ts` (its frame positioned).
- A long virtualised list (the contacts) closes ranks without its rows' exits. A wall replaced whole just goes; cards scrolled into the window never animate.

**Screens and set pieces.**
- Going deeper on the phone (a collection, a link, Réglages) takes Android's own transition (`animation: 'default'`); a note or a full-screen form opens with `fade_from_bottom`.
- A contact's card opens in three beats as the screen slides in: the face settling from 0.94, then the ways to reach them, then what is known, each rising 8 a beat later (250). A face starred elsewhere settles into the favourites' row as the others make room.
- An empty state's collage is laid down as the view opens, piece by piece in drawing order (`collageEntrances`): each cut-out drifts in from just outside its place (10, from 0.92) and turns 12° into it; the paper sheet slides up 14 and settles from 7° steeper; 420 each on emphasized decelerate, 70 apart. Reduce Motion keeps the web's fades and shows the phone's still.

### Inputs / Fields

### Forms
The same in every app, on both clients.
- **Labels stay** above their field (metaStrong); never a placeholder as a label; an example goes in the help. Exception: an event's rows, each led by its glyph in a 24 slot and named for screen readers (place: « Ajouter un lieu » in link ink).
- **Say it once:** drop a word the screen already shows, keeping it for screen readers: no title over self-named fields (the contact form's Prénom, Nom de famille, Entreprise, Fonction), no field name under a section that names it (« Anniversaire »), no word over a choice whose look says it (swatches, cut-outs, day and hour chips). Help never restates its label; it keeps what cannot be seen (a format, a cost, a consequence).
- **One column** under section titles; two short fields side by side only when read together (postal code and town, day and time); a field's width matches its answer's length.
- **Validation** (`core/services/formChecks`, `useForm`):
  - silent while a fresh value is typed; checked when a non-empty field is left;
  - an empty field is "missing" only after the form was sent;
  - a field in error re-checks at each keystroke and clears at once; a valid field being edited waits until left;
  - sending shows every problem, focuses the first (scrolled to mid-screen) and counts them by the actions (`FormMessage`: « 2 champs sont à corriger. », `error` glyph, danger ink), announced to screen readers.
- **The primary action is never disabled to say why.** It sinks to the well only with nothing to act on, or while busy (spins, refuses a second press).
- **Server refusals** show under the field concerned, the typed value kept, else on the form's line; never a slip alone.
- **Words:** the action names the act (« Créer », « Ajouter », « S'abonner », « Enregistrer »); a problem says what is wrong and how to fix it.
- **Saving, one way per form:** a setting applies at once (a slip to undo when it matters); a typed setting applies on « Appliquer », Enter or blur; a record's form saves on its button (web: also ⌘/Ctrl+Enter, ⌘/Ctrl+S).
- **Actions:** a phone form screen is a full-screen dialog (× at the top bar's start, `closes`; title; « Enregistrer » at its end). A sheet pins them at its foot (`FormFoot`, `footEdge`: « Annuler » text, the primary filled, last), riding on the keyboard. A web dialog keeps them in its still foot (`DialogFrame`); a web form page in a foot stuck to the window's bottom.
- **Keyboard (phone):** a one-line field's action is « Suivant » (next field, keyboard up); the last is « OK » (closes it, or sends a sheet of one or two fields). Buttons work at the first press with the keyboard up.
- **Keys (web):** Enter in a one-line field sends; Shift+Enter or a multiline field breaks the line; Escape closes a dialog.
- **Unsaved changes** (`useUnsaved`): leaving the page or screen, or closing the dialog or sheet, asks « Abandonner les modifications ? » (« Continuer à modifier », « Abandonner » in danger). Forms that save as they go never ask.
- **Typed date** (`core/services/dateTyping`): one digit-keyboard field; « / » comes after a part's two digits or a digit that cannot start two (day from 4, month from 2); a separator typed after one digit pads it; backspace over a separator takes the digit before; a paste is laid out; an inner edit settles on blur. Help: « JJ/MM/AAAA, année facultative »; examples use a day past 12 (27/03).
- **Opening:** a form made for typing (new label, link, question) focuses its first field; a record's form opens with the keyboard down, unless new.

### Scroll Edges
A bar marks where content goes under it; a fade only says content continues. Both clients, every app.
- **Bars and heads take a hard line, never a fade:** headers and top bars, sticky page heads, sheet heads, dialog heads and feet, sticky form feet, Agenda's period bar and day heads, the note editor's bars, Pages' block bar, composers. Each paints the ground (its slice, or its own surface), opaque, and draws one line: 1, hairline ink, full width. No shadow, blur or mask.
- **Head line:** only once content is under it, eased in over the first px of scroll (150); none at rest.
- **Foot line** (`footEdge`, `FormFoot`): always drawn; one per footer (the first row of a two-row footer).
- **Fade:** the eased 24 dissolve (cosine ease-in-out, nine stops, as strong as the content beyond, full at 24), only:
  - on sideways scrollers (chip rows, strips, carousels, a table's sides), at the overflowing side, while it overflows;
  - on a vertical scroller capped inside a card, panel, menu or popover with no bar at that edge, where it is cut.
- **Never a fade** under a bar, where content leaves the screen (page foot, status bar, keyboard, gesture bar), or on a field or code well.
- **Phone:** use the edge scrollers (`EdgeScrollView`, `EdgeFlatList`, `EdgeSectionList`, `ScrollEdges`, `SheetScroll`, `SheetFlatList`, `RevealScrollView`, Réglages' scroll), never a plain vertical `ScrollView`/`FlatList`: they tell the head above (`TopBar`, `SearchHeader`, a sheet's or dialog's head) how far content has gone under it.
- **Web** (`components/scrollEdges.ts`): the page's scroll draws the header's line (`usePageEdges`) and a stuck form foot's (`useStuckFootEdge`); an inner scroller draws its bar's line from `--edge-above-k` / `--edge-below-k`; fades are opt-in; tables use `useHiddenSides`.
- **The caret's line stays clear of bars, and nothing jumps:** a tapped field does not move on focus; one focused otherwise scrolls just enough to show its caret; when the keyboard rises the scroller glides the least it can to the caret, never to the field's top; a line break moves the text one line. The edge scrollers handle this on both clients (web: `watchScrollEdges`, `scroll-padding`): never add a scroll-on-focus.
- **Down to the screen's edge:** a scroller with nothing under it (drawer list, an action-less sheet body or dialog, a picker's closing list) runs behind the gesture bar and keeps that bar's room inside the end of its content, never as an empty band of panel (phone: `useFootRoom`; web: `.sheetBody`, `data-scroller`). With actions, the room goes under the actions.

### Empty States
Every place that can hold nothing says so; never a blank.
- **A whole place** (a screen, a view, a list that is the screen, an app's search with no result): `EmptyState`: a collage (`design/collage.ts`: `notes`, `reminders`, `archive`, `trash`, `label`, `welcome`, `search`), a title, what goes there, one action. Pieces drift in from just outside their place and turn into it, 420 each, 70 apart. No result: the `search` collage and what that app's search looks through.
- **A small place** (a picker's or menu's list, a sheet's answers, a page block, a search in a sheet or popover, a row of Réglages): `EmptyLine`: an optional glyph (20, muted), one sentence in muted ink at the surrounding rows' size, an optional quiet action. It fades in and rises 8 (250, decelerate), 120 late so a list that fills at once never flashes it; a new sentence replays it, more typing with the same result does not (`replayKey`). Carnet's dashed panels settle the same way (`settleIn`).
- **Reduce Motion:** web keeps the fades, no travel; phone shows them still.
- **Words:** what is empty, then what fills it or why; the same sentence on both clients.

**Previews and the quick window**
- **Previews:** each widget has its own layout for the launcher's picker (`widget_*_preview.xml`, or the live layout when it draws without a list): a launcher fills no list there. Samples are short enough to read whole in the picker's narrower frame.
- **The quick window:** a translucent window over the home screen that is its own scrim (150, decelerate; `SheetScrim` off), the app's own sheet rising in it: "Classer un lien" (`LinkCaptureSheet`), a list's new items, a picker as list rows. A link added closes it at once; a slip says where it went (1.6s).

## Do's and Don'ts

The checklist; each line's detail is in its section above.

### Do:
- **Do** take every radius from `RADII` by role, the same for every item of a role, concentric when nested.
- **Do** keep a control's radius under half its height (the switch aside).
- **Do** use the pebble for what is chosen, captured or kept and for glimpsed pictures, neighbours on different turns; let it give and spring back while sheets stay still.
- **Do** take every colour from the palette through the theme or `var(--…)`, and the primary, the accent and the ground from the appearance resolved for the theme; a pinned bar shows the ground's slice (`GroundSlice`), never a flat tone of it.
- **Do** fill actions in terracotta, write links in deep terracotta, and mark capture, the chosen, focus and switches in matcha; keep tonal buttons matcha wash.
- **Do** edge cards, chips and bars with the 1px hairline; menus, dialogs, sheet lips and field-like strips with the outline; a control drawn by its edge alone with `edge`.
- **Do** hover and press inside the control's own shape: 6% of its ink under a hovering pointer, 10% under the press (a link-coloured control: 8% and 12%).
- **Do** lay a screen on the ground (`Ground` on the phone, `var(--ground-paint)` on the web), with grain on it and on every sheet, one layer per surface.
- **Do** lift paper with the shared lifts only: Card at rest, Raised for hover, Float for sheets, dialogs, menus, the drawer and the slip.
- **Do** size a board card from its content with `layoutCard`, never by measuring it.
- **Do** keep a link's own picture in its 4:5 frame.
- **Do** settle arrivals on decelerate within 150, 250 or 300ms, from 0.9 or more; send departures off faster on accelerate, and keep an overlay mounted until its exit has played.
- **Do** keep Signika alone, set by family on the phone, with the night grade after dark.
- **Do** draw any new icon in Onde (`tools/icons/onde.mjs`), in one neutral ink.

### Don't:
- **Don't** give an item random corners, a seeded outline, bowed sides or a radius outside the scale, or round a container past `CORNER_CAP`.
- **Don't** put text on a pebble.
- **Don't** put a stone in a navigation row other than the chosen place's (on the phone, none: the chosen row lies on its wash).
- **Don't** float or round the drawer; it runs the full height, square and flush.
- **Don't** paint chrome, text or a whole note in a pigment, lay a pigment on the matcha fill, or ornament chrome.
- **Don't** use matcha for a filled action, terracotta for selection or focus, or any green but matcha.
- **Don't** add a third tone to Carnet's own ground, or let a colour a person picks reach the screen unfitted.
- **Don't** bounce anything but the snip's landing and the pebble's spring back.
- **Don't** set uppercase anywhere but the stamp.
- **Don't** colour an icon or a checkbox, give an icon a face, bring in an icon library, or draw anything behind an icon's line but its control's pebble tint.
- **Don't** cast a shadow behind an icon, emblem, stone, swatch, pebble or illustration.
- **Don't** fade a disabled button's pigment or opacity; it sinks to a well.
- **Don't** tilt, lift, translate or grow a control under the pointer (static turns such as the stamp, a stone's emblem and the ghost emblem are drawn, not hovers).
- **Don't** stack grain on a nested surface, or lay it on a photo.
- **Don't** hard-code a hex, a radius or a shadow in a component; the web reads generated custom properties, the phone reads the theme.
