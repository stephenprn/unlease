# Design System: Carnet

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Galet et feuille: calm sheets resting on pebbles"**

Carnet is a notebook of calm paper sheets resting on pebbles, after its own mark (a beige card on a matcha pebble). Every container is a smooth sheet with one continuous corner, the same for every item of a role and at every size, so a hundred notes read as one quiet wall. The brand's pebble is the one organic form: it marks what is chosen, captured or kept (the capture pebble, the chosen place, the open info, a collection's emblem on its stone, a picture glimpsed), and it never holds text. The palette is habits-garden's: paper ground, cream sheets, warm near-black ink, terracotta fills, a matcha accent and fourteen earth pigments. That is Carnet's own look; on each device a person may choose another pair of colours (a palette, or each picked precisely) and another ground (a preset, a colour, or a gradient they generate, grainy or not), always fitted so every ratio of the system still holds (`design/appearance.ts`).

Density is Keep's: two columns on the phone, as many as fit on the desktop, each card exactly as tall as its content. One humanist face (Signika) at fixed sizes, weight carrying rank. Depth is paper resting on paper: a hairline edge, one soft light, quiet grain never stacked, the whole on a ground of two tones of the wall, lighter at the top, under its own grain. The icons are Carnet's own, Onde: one even ink line where every straight stroke takes one soft wave and frames swell a little, clean at 16px. Motion is short and settling; a pebble pressed gives rounder and springs back, sheets never change shape; the snip is the one authored moment. Both clients paint from the same data in `design/` (palette and its grounds, shapes, outline, pebbles, backgrounds, emblems, glyphs, collage, mark), so the phone (Android, primary) and the web twin cannot drift. The research behind the corner, the radius scale and keeping organic forms off text is in `docs/DESIGN-RESEARCH.md`.

Rejected by the direction contract and the owner (2026-10-22): per-item random outlines, wobbling or bowed edges, organic shapes holding text, ornamented chrome, Keep's plain rounded rectangles, the old metro's navy signage. Pinned by the owner the same evening: icons in one ink, original and gently wavy but clean (never coloured, never faces or kawaii), and a ground of two tones only (beige to a darker beige; grey to a darker grey at night), which stays Carnet's own ground: since 2026-10-27 a person may lay another on their device (Réglages > Fond), never one that breaks a ratio. Kept owner pins: green only as the matcha accent; icons and checkboxes neutral on any note colour; nothing behind an icon's line but a pebble tint; no shadow behind any icon, emblem or illustration; hovers and presses stay inside the control and never tilt or jump.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Sheets: one continuous corner (Figma's, smoothing 0.6) from a short radius scale by role, capped at 0.3 of the shorter side, nested concentrically, a hairline edge.
- One pebble: the mark's stone, stretched to its box and turned one of four ways, never under text; pressed, it gives toward round and springs back.
- Controls are soft sheets: their radius stays under half their height, never a capsule.
- Terracotta for filled actions and links; matcha for capture, the chosen, focus and switches.
- Earth pigments only on emblems, label tags, stones' washes and note washes; a note may also wear a soft mesh gradient, and a paper under either: three in faint ink (dots, grid, lines) and four in a pigment's ink (Seyès, millimetre, isometric dots, flecks).
- A hover or press is a tint laid inside the control's own shape, never a ripple past it, a tilt or a jump.
- Onde icons, Carnet's own: one even 1.9 line with round ends, a soft wave in every straight stroke, frames that swell a little, breathing circles; eleven glyphs take their solid form when their control is selected.
- Cut-outs hand-cut after Matisse: emblems and the label tag, flat, no cast shadow.
- A two-tone ground fixed to the window, lighter at the top, under a slightly stronger grain; quiet grain on every sheet, never stacked.
- System messages on a charcoal slip, dark in both modes.

## Colors

habits-garden's warm paper neutrals, a terracotta action family, a matcha accent family and fourteen earth pigments. Every value lives in `design/palette.ts`; its contrast test (`__tests__/palette.test.ts`) is the authority. Réglages calls the matcha family the primary colour ("Principale") and the terracotta family the accent ("Accent"): both can change per device, with the ground (see The Person's Colours and Ground, below). Pigment keys keep their old names because they sync (`yellow` is Safran, `grey` is Sauge); collections are still "lines" in code (`LineColor`, `LINES`, `--line-<key>`). Night values are the `night-*` tokens; the web switches them on `prefers-color-scheme` or `<html data-theme>`. The night ground is a warm black-grey from habits-garden's ink (OKLCH chroma under 0.01, warm hues 48 to 68), not its moss.

### Primary
- **Terracotta** (action, on-action): filled buttons, the one primary action on a surface.
- **Deep Terracotta** (link): links, text buttons, the "+ Collection" label, the reading track's scissors.
- **Peach** (highlight): text selection on both clients, and search matches (on the web with an underline in the text's own ink); at night a deep rust.

### Secondary
- **Deep Matcha** (focus): focus rings, carets and selection handles, the selected card ring, the chosen segment's tick, the selection bar's edge, the ring of a picker's chosen finish or emblem, switches.
- **Matcha Wash** (action-container, on-action-container): tonal buttons (kept matcha: a neutral tonal button read as disabled), the selection bar, a selected icon button's pebble, the chosen navigation place's pebble behind a glyph, the info card. A clear step darker than the paper by day. No pigment is ever laid on it.
- **Leaf Matcha** (leaf, on-leaf): the capture pebble, the selection tick pebble, a picker's check; never text.
- **Mark Matcha and Mark Beige** (mark-tile, mark-card): the mark's two colours, the same by day and night (`MARK_COLORS`): the pebble, and the card the leaf is cut from; nowhere else. Another primary takes the pebble (`markColors`): itself when the card stands off it as far as on Carnet's own mark (3.4:1), else its hue deepened the least it must; the card stays beige.
- **Slip Matcha** (slip-action): "Annuler" and every action on the slip, and the slip's focus ring.

### Tertiary
- **The Pigments** (pigment-*, night lifts): Safran, Orange brûlé, Rouge, Framboise, Bordeaux, Lavande, Violet, Bleu, Sarcelle, Vert, Olive, Ocre, Brun, Sauge. A collection's emblem, a label's tag, a colour swatch's edge, the snip's cut once filed. The slip is dark in both themes, so a pigment drawn on it takes its night lift (the phone's `theme.slipLine`, the web's `[data-surface='slip']` tokens). Ink on a pigment is whichever of the two inks contrasts more (on-pigment-dark or on-pigment-light; night always night-on-pigment). Sauge belongs to "Non classé".
- **Washes** (wash-*): a coloured note's paper and a collection's stone (`--tint`, `theme.line[key].tint`), one OKLCH hue and chroma per pigment (`WASHES`), lightness 0.936 by day; at night lightness 0.345 (a step above the night card) with the chroma cut to 0.82 of the day's (at most 0.045). Ink holds 7:1 on every wash.
- **Text inks** (text-*, night-text-*; `--line-<key>-text`): a pigment as words on a page, the person's coloured text and code's colours, held at 4.5:1 on every paper, the well and every wash. A page's highlighted words lie on the pigment's wash.
- **Gradients** (`backgrounds.ts`): eight soft mesh washes a note can wear instead of a pigment (Aube, Lagon, Matcha latte, Abricot, Brume lavande, Nacre, Sous-bois, Terre cuite), pale by day, deep at night, ink 7:1 on every colour.

### The Person's Colours and Ground
Per device, never synced (`Appearance` in `design/appearance.ts`, stored with the device's settings), resolved for each theme by `resolveAppearance`: the palette's roles with the primary's and the accent's, and the ground's paint. The phone reads them through the theme (`theme.color`, `theme.ground`), the web as custom properties laid on `<html>` through the CSSOM for the theme on screen (the page's policy refuses inline stylesheets; `web/notes/src/ui/appearanceStyle.ts`), the stylesheet keeping Carnet's own.
- **The primary** (leaf, on-leaf, focus, action-container, on-action-container, slip-action) and **the accent** (action, on-action, link, highlight). Eight pairs that go together (`PALETTES`: Carnet's matcha and terracotta, Sarcelle, Myrtille, Glycine, Pivoine, Mimosa, Ardoise, Mousse), or each colour picked precisely. The picked colour is the capture pebble's (primary) or a filled button's (accent) as it is when an ink holds 4.5:1 on it (and, for the accent, 3:1 off every surface); every other role takes its hue at the depth the palette gives matcha or terracotta, moved in lightness the least it must to read where it is drawn, on the ground chosen too. Carnet's pair gives back the palette's own values. Signals stay theirs: ok, waiting, danger, the stamp, the mark's card. The mark's pebble follows the primary (`markColors`), in the apps and in the web's tab; the launcher, the widgets and the install icons keep Carnet's matcha.

Every ground has a day and a night: a colour reads on the ground when every ink, edge, default action and pigment of the wall keeps its ratio on it, and at night a card still stands 1.15:1 off it; a picked colour that does not is moved in lightness (pale by day, deep at night), and a generated one keeps a margin so the browser's blends between two never cross the line (`fitGround`, `gradientPaint`). The ground's own grain is a switch of its own ("Grain sur le fond").

### Neutral
- **The Ground** (ground-top, ground-bottom; `GROUNDS`, `groundGradient` in `palette.ts`): what every screen lies on, two tones from the top of the window to its foot, cream to a warmer sand by day (#F4F0E2 to #E8D9B5, the foot as deep as the pigments allow: Sarcelle, Safran and Sauge keep 3:1 there) and grey to a near-black grey at night (#292725 to #11100F), fixed while the content scrolls, under the ground's grain. The drawer and the bottom bar paint the same ground (the bar the slice it covers), the header and top bars show it through; the browser chrome takes its top.
- **Paper Ground** (wall): the ground's flat colour: sheets, dialogs and side sheets, and the fallback under the gradient.
- **Cream Card** (paper): cards, chips, menus, the chosen segment, the paper pebble under a chosen cut-out in a picker. At night a card stands a clear step off both tones of the ground (1.15:1 at its top, 1.5:1 at its foot), so a sheet never melts into it.
- **Field Cream** (field): text fields and the search strip, a step lighter than a card by day.
- **Well** (paper-high): link thumbnails and a link's image frame, link facts, a photo still loading, the trash banner, a disabled button, a switch's off track and the Keep drop zone.
- **Charcoal Slip** (slip, slip-high, on-slip, on-slip-muted): the snackbar and the snip panel.

Hairline edges cards, chips and bars; outline is the firmer edge of menus, dialogs, the drawer's open side, sheet lips, the search, add-link and new-label strips, a link's image frame and footer chips, and draws the sheet handle and reading dashes; track sinks the segmented control; dashed edges the "+ Collection".

### Named Rules
**The Pigment Belongs To Cut-Outs Rule.** Pigments fill emblems, label tags and the snip's cut; a note and a stone wear the pigment's wash or a gradient, never the pigment itself. Chrome stays paper, terracotta and matcha. On a page, the person's own words may take a pigment's text ink or its wash, and code takes text inks: content, never chrome.

**The Terracotta Acts, Matcha Chooses Rule.** Terracotta fills and links; matcha marks capture, what is chosen, what has focus and what is switched on.

**The Green Is Only Matcha Rule.** Green appears only as matcha (the accent, and the sync dot's deep matcha): no neutral, night ground or veil carries a green cast, and icons and checkboxes stay neutral ink on any note colour. A person's primary takes matcha's place in every one of its roles and nowhere else.

**The Paper Under Pigment Rule.** A pigment never sits on the matcha fill. The chosen navigation place puts a collection's cut-out or a label's tag on its own wash pebble, a glyph on matcha; a picker marks a chosen cut-out with a paper pebble ringed in matcha.

**The Contrast Test Rule.** A new colour, wash or gradient enters `palette.ts`, `backgrounds.ts` or `appearance.ts` and passes its contrast test, or it does not ship (a person's own colours pass the same ratios at runtime, every palette, ground, random colour and generated gradient tested in `__tests__/appearance.test.ts`): ink 7:1 and the second ink 4.5:1 on every paper, wash and gradient; `edge` 3:1 on every ground; every ink, edge and pigment of the wall at its own ratio on both tones of the ground; a well 1.15 against the wall, the ground's top and 1.1 against a card; a disabled label 4.5:1 in its well; the sync dots 3:1 on the strip.

## Elevation & Depth

Paper off the ground: a 1px hairline edge plus a soft, warm two-layer lift by day and a single dark lift at night (`LIFTS` in `palette.ts`, the same CSS syntax on both clients). A container clipped to its outline on the web casts its lift from an unclipped wrapper as drop shadows (`--drop-*`, the blur halved), since a clip cuts a box-shadow off. The grain is the second depth cue: the ground's is fixed behind everything, each sheet carries its own between its colour and its content, never stacked on a nested surface.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`0 1px 1px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.05), 0 2px 6px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.05)`; night `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`): cards, chips, the search strip, the composer, the chosen segment.
- **Raised** (`0 2px 3px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.08), 0 6px 14px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.09)`; night `0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`): a card or the composer under the pointer.
- **Float** (`0 4px 10px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.12), 0 14px 30px rgba(46, 43, 37, 0.14)`; night `0 6px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`): sheets, dialogs, menus, the drawer, the slip.
- **Bar lift** (the `shadow` colour, cast upwards: `0 -1px 3px` at 70% and `0 -6px 16px` at 60%): the web bottom bar alone.

### Named Rules
**The Shadow Moves, Text Never Does Rule.** Hover lifts a card from Card to Raised; selection is a ring laid over the sheet; content never shifts, and nothing tilts or jumps under the pointer.

**The Flat Cut-Out Rule.** Nothing cast or drawn on the paper has a shadow: emblems, stones, collage pieces, swatches, the tick pebble and the capture pebble lie flat. Lifts belong to sheets of paper.

**The Quiet Grain Rule.** Grain at 0.16 by day and 0.08 at night on sheets (`GRAIN` in `web/notes/build/tokens.ts`; `grain` in the phone theme), tiled at 200 from a 256 procedural speckle; one layer per surface, never stacked, never on photos. The ground carries its own visible film grain on the web: every pixel of a 256 tile moves a little (`web/notes/public/ground-grain.png`, strength baked into the file, both from `tools/textures/build_grain.py`), laid at its own size over the two tones as one more background layer (`--ground-paint`), so the header, the bars and the drawer that paint the ground paint it whole; the phone lays the same grain (`mobile/notes/assets/textures/ground-grain.png`, its pixels doubled, one to one on the screen's pixels, as the widgets do: `Grain ground`) over its ground, its drawer and its bottom bar. Réglages turns each grain off on that device: the sheets' in Apparence ("Grain du papier": `html[data-grain='off']` on the web, the paper `Grain` drawing nothing on the phone), the ground's in Fond ("Grain sur le fond", the appearance's own `grain`: `html[data-ground-grain='off']`, `theme.groundGrain`); a surface without is flat colour. Home-screen widgets keep theirs (they follow the system, not the app).

## Shapes

Two forms, and only two: the sheet and the pebble (`design/shapes.ts`, `outline.ts`, `pebbles.ts`).

**Sheets.** Every container and control is a sheet with one continuous corner: Figma's construction at smoothing 0.6 (`SMOOTHING`, `squircleSegments`), a short circular arc between two easing curves, so the bend grows from nothing and no hump shows. One radius per role from a short scale (`RADII`, px on the web, dp on the phone): detail 5, small 9, chip 12, row 12, frame 12, control 13, bar 15, menu 18, card 20, panel 22, dialog 28; the segment is the bar less its 4 inset (11), and a shape nested in another is concentric with it (`concentric`: the parent's radius less the padding). No corner takes more than 0.3 of the box's shorter side (`CORNER_CAP`), so a one-line note stays a sheet, never a pill. The same radius serves every item of a role at every size: no per-item seed, no random corner, no bowed side. A sheet standing on a screen edge keeps those corners square: the bottom sheet (28 on its two top corners), a side sheet on the right edge (28 on its open side), the drawer (square, flush, full height). The info card is a panel whose corner by its pointer is tight (5), so the pointer stands on a straight edge (`bubble`).

Both clients draw the same corner. The web clips a container to its path (`useOutline`, `OutlinedBox`, or `data-outline="kind"` for a page's blocks: `card`, `panel`, `frame`, `menu`), draws its edge along it from inside (`OutlineEdge`) and its focus ring 4 out; a control takes `border-radius: var(--cut-*)`, made continuous with `corner-shape: squircle` where the browser has it, its radii scaled by `--corner-scale` (1.84 there, 1 elsewhere) so a browser without it (Firefox, the owner's) draws a round corner of the same depth. The phone draws it in its native view (`Organic`, Android's `OrganicView` with `Squircle.kt`, number for number), from `corners(SHAPES.role)` or `outline="kind"`; a plain `View` draws circles.

**The pebble.** The stone the mark's card lies on (`MARK_PEBBLE`), stretched to touch every side of its box, turned one of four ways (`PEBBLE_TURNS`): neighbours differ (`pebbleTurn(i)`), and a collection keeps its own stone for life (`pebbleTurnOf(id)`). Pressed, it gives 0.62 of the way toward the circle through its nodes and springs back: on the web `clip-path: shape(...)` in percentages (`--pebble-N`, `--pebble-N-pressed`; `data-pebble` clips the element, `data-pebble-under` paints it on `::before` so the focus ring stays whole, `data-still` keeps it from giving), in 90ms on `--ease-out` and back in 380ms on `--ease-spring`; on the phone `Organic` or `Tappable` with `pebble` (`pebbleNumbers`), the native press morph on `given`, 90ms decelerate in, 380ms spring back. Reduced motion holds it still. The pebble serves the capture pebble, an icon button's tint, a checkbox's tint, the chosen navigation place, a collection's stone, swatches and finish tiles, the selection tick, the open info and pictures glimpsed (a collection card's thumbnails). It never holds text. Dots (the sync dot, a picture's page dots) are circles.

**Cut-outs.** Hand-cut after Matisse, from data: irregular outlines on a 100 grid (`emblems.ts`, drafted by `tools/emblems/draft_emblems.py`). Fourteen emblems: feuille, étoile, soleil, fleur, trèfle, galet (even-odd), cœur, lune, goutte, nuage, vague, poisson (even-odd), oiseau, arche. "Non classé" always wears the tray ("bac"). A label wears a hand-cut paper tag (`LABEL_TAG_PATH` in a 96 by 68 box) drawn by both clients' `LabelMark`. Empty-state collages sit on a 240 by 150 board and are laid down as the view opens, piece by piece in drawing order (`collageEntrances`): each cut-out drifts in from just outside its place (10, from 0.92) and turns 12° into it, the paper sheet slides up 14 and settles from 7° steeper, 420 each on emphasized decelerate, 70 apart; Reduce Motion keeps the web's fades and shows the phone's still. The mark: a beige card turned -6°, a leaf and stem cut out of it in one even-odd path, on a matcha pebble (`mark.ts`, `MARK_COLORS`; in the apps the pebble takes the primary, `markColors`); the card path alone is the one-colour silhouette; every launcher, widget and web icon draws these paths.

**The Calm Wall Rule.** One continuous corner per role from `RADII`, the same for every item and every size, capped at 0.3 of the shorter side and concentric when nested. Never a seed, a random corner, a bowed side or a radius invented outside the scale.

**The Soft Sheet Control Rule.** A control's radius stays under half its height (a button 13 at 48, a field 13 at 52, the strip 15 at 54): it reads as a soft sheet, never a capsule, so Firefox without `corner-shape` draws what Chromium draws. The one exception is a switch: its track keeps the universal capsule and its thumb a circle, the shape everyone reads as a toggle.

**The Pebble Holds No Text Rule.** The pebble marks what is chosen, captured or kept, and crops glimpsed pictures; text always sits on a sheet. A link's own picture keeps its 4:5 frame, so products compare whole.

## Motion

Material 3's curves and one set of durations on both clients (`mobile/notes/src/ui/theme/motion.ts`; `--ease-*`, `--duration-*` in `web/notes/src/ui/styles/base.css`): 150 for feedback, 250 for a state change, 300 for something large arriving (a sheet, the drawer, the slip), never longer outside the snip, the pebble's spring back and an empty state's collage being laid down. Arrivals on emphasized decelerate, state changes on standard, departures on emphasized accelerate and shorter (150, or 200 for a sheet, the drawer and the slip). A press tint fades out in 150 once the finger lifts. Nothing grows from nothing: an arrival starts at 0.9 or more. Overlays grow from where they were opened, play their exit, and their host stays mounted while it runs.

The pebble's give is the signature interaction: 90 in, 380 back on a spring (`--ease-spring`, `cubic-bezier(0.42, 1.67, 0.21, 0.9)`; the phone's `PathInterpolator` with the same numbers). Sheets never change shape.

Gestures follow the finger 1:1 and hand its speed on: back to rest on M3's spatial spring (`spatial`, `spatialSlow` for a sheet or the drawer), or thrown off keeping the hand's speed (`throwTo`). A swipe that will act says so before it is let go: its threshold clicks (`mobile/notes/src/platform/haptics.ts`) and what it uncovers turns full strength; a long press that picks something up clicks too.

In lists, something leaving fades where it was, the rest close ranks (250, standard), something arriving settles in once they have made room. A wall replaced whole just goes; cards scrolled into the window never animate. Going deeper on the phone takes Android's own transition (`animation: 'default'`); a note opens with `fade_from_bottom`. Reduce Motion: movement goes; the web keeps fades and colour changes, the phone cuts.

The picker searches the whole catalogue by name and keyword in the languages Réglages ticks (French, English; `emojiCatalogue.json` from `tools/emoji/build_emoji.mjs`, loaded when a picker first opens), shows the everyday ones then Unicode's groups when nothing is typed, and takes a pasted emoji as it is.
- **Tables:** a cell's whole height is its target: its hover and press tint the whole cell, and a press anywhere in it opens it. Open, the whole cell is the field with a 2px matcha ring drawn over everything in it, the text in its own place (the editor lies over an invisible copy, so the row grows as lines are typed). A strict type (number, date, e-mail, link, telephone; `readCellInput` in `notes/services/table.ts`) takes only what fits it: Enter on anything else keeps the cell open, its ring in danger and what it expects under the text ("Ce n’est pas une adresse e-mail. Exemple : nom@exemple.fr"); leaving it keeps the old value and the slip offers "Corriger". A cell that stopped fitting when its column changed type keeps its words, a danger mark beside them.
- **Blocks:** selected blocks wear a matcha wash with a 1.5 focus edge; drops show a matcha bar. Callouts are panels (22) on the well or a pigment's wash; code blocks, bookmarks, pictures, videos, PDFs and file cards are frames (12); embeds of notes are cards (20) on the note's own paper; options in tables and mentions take the detail radius (5).
- **Captions:** one quiet line under a code block, a table, a media, a bookmark or an embed (14/20, muted ink), typed in place. It shows once written, or while it has the caret ("Ajouter une légende" in the block's menu puts it there), and hides again when left empty.
- **Menus by the caret:** "/" and "@" in the menu's paper and Float lift, kept 8 inside what the window shows; rows 36 under a mouse, 44 under a finger. « [[ » opens the same search for pages alone and cites the one picked as a mention.
- **Dates:** a date is one piece of the text, a mention's chip holding the calendar glyph and the day as it reads today, in ink a weight up (« Aujourd’hui », « Demain », « Hier », a weekday within the week, « jeu. 17 décembre » further off, the year when it is not this one, the time after it when it has one); shared, copied or read by Claude it is written in words that stay true (« jeudi 17 décembre 2026 »). It comes from « / » (« Date », today) or « @ », where a day typed (« demain », « lundi », « 12/11 », « 12/11/2026 14h ») comes first. A click on it (on the phone, the caret beside it and « Modifier la date », first on the bar) opens its editor: the date in title, the usual days as small paper chips (« Aujourd’hui », « Demain », « Lundi prochain »; the one it is on in matcha wash with a matcha edge), the month between two chevrons over the weekdays' initials and its days as calm 36 cells (40 under a finger; today ringed 1.5 in matcha at 600, the chosen day in matcha wash), « Heure » as a switch row, then the time (a time field on the web; on the phone the hours 6 to 22 and the quarters as chips), and « Retirer la date » in danger ink. A popover by the chip on the web (the sheet under 720px), a sheet on the phone.
- **Emoji and keys:** « : » and a word's first two letters offer the emoji it names, in the languages Réglages ticks: a small menu by the caret on the web (300 wide), eight on the keyboard's bar on the phone; a press or Enter puts it in place of the word. « Touches » sets words as keys: on the web each run one little key on paper (detail radius, an outline ring and a deeper foot, 600 at 0.86em); on the phone Signika a weight up and a little smaller on paper, a field drawing no border round a span. Code stays the monospace on the well.
- **Table of contents:** no frame, the page read as a document: its headings as quiet rows in the second ink (32 high, 40 under a finger, the tab radius for hover 6% and press 10%), each 16 further in by its depth; a press takes the page to the heading. With no heading yet, one meta line says so.
- **Drawing block:** a frame (12) at the column's width, as tall as the drawing's proportions, the strokes in the page's inks, its caption under it as for media; empty, a quiet panel with the draw glyph and « Cliquez pour dessiner » (« Touchez pour dessiner » on the phone). It opens the drawing editor: on the web a dialog as large as a drawing wants (the sheet under 720px) with the note's canvas and tools and « Terminé »; on the phone a whole screen, « Dessin », its undo and redo in the top bar. Each stroke goes in the page's undo as it is drawn.
- **Rhythm:** a paragraph keeps 8 above and below, so two read as two, while a list's items stay close; a boxed block keeps 12 above and below; headings take more room above than below (32, 28, 20 and 16; 4 below).
- **Demander à Claude:** present only once the Administration turns the questions on (and Claude has a key), and never intrusive: one quiet `sparkle` icon button beside the page's menu (the page bar from 720px, the top bar on a phone and under 720px, between Rétablir and the menu), pressed while its conversation is open, and « Demander à Claude » first in the page's menu; nothing opens, pulses or badges on its own. From 720px the conversation docks beside the page as a panel (22, float lift, paper and grain, min(400, 38%) wide, full height under the header), the page's column narrowing beside it; it stays while a source is followed to another page, and Escape or its × closes it. On a phone and under 720px it is a tall bottom sheet (no ×: handle, scrim, Back), which a source closes (the conversation is kept). Inside: the sparkle and « Demander à Claude » (title) over « À propos de « page » » (meta), a broom for a new conversation once there is one; the scope on a segmented track (« Cette page », « Sous-pages » when it has some, « Toutes » when allowed) over meta lines saying what Claude reads (pages, words, the cost at most), what was left out for room and how many pages are kept away; the conversation scrolls, starters (small text buttons on the web, edged chips on the phone) at its foot while it is empty; a question is the person's own words right-aligned on the field's tone (tab radius); an answer keeps its paragraphs and "- " lists, each claim followed by its sources' numbers on the action's wash (detail radius, 11 tabular), then the sources numbered in order of first use (the page's path in metaStrong, the cited words in meta, two lines at most, the whole row pressable), the web's as links, and « Copier » and « Ajouter à la page » (text buttons: a toggle titled with the question at the end of the page, the cited pages as mentions under it). Waiting is the scissors' track with « Claude lit n pages… » and « Annuler »; a refusal is one danger line and « Réessayer ». The question field sits at the foot under a hairline, one line growing to five, Enter asks, its send pebble beside it, and « Chercher aussi sur le web » as a switch row when allowed. A page kept away from Claude (its menu: « Garder hors de Claude » / « Montrer à Claude ») shows no sparkle; its sub-pages neither. A source opens its page at the block, lit with the selection's wash a moment.
- **Phone:** the pages' home mirrors the board (the search strip searching the pages alone, "Récentes" from five pages, "Toutes les pages" as rows, a line to the pages in the Corbeille, the bottom bar whose pebble makes a page). A page: a top bar (back, its title once the page's own has scrolled under it, undo, redo, Plus d'actions: a sub-page, a move, a copy, share as Markdown, the bin), the breadcrumb on one line (the first page, "…", the parent), the emoji (64 by 60 pebble) or "Ajouter une icône", the title, the blocks. Text kinds are typed in native fields drawing their marks (a mention as its name in link ink on the veil, `core/services/richDisplay.ts`); a kind changed keeps its field and the keyboard. While a block holds the caret a bar rides on the keyboard (52, the ground, a hairline): with the caret + (the "/" menu as a sheet by section), Transformer en…, out, in, up, down, its actions; with the caret beside a date « Modifier la date » first; with words selected Gras, Italique, Souligné, Barré, Code, Touches, Couleur (text ink and highlight), Lien; while « :word » is typed, the emoji it names. "/" and "@" typed after a space open the insert sheet and the mention search, « [[ » the search for a page to cite. Held down, a table, a media, a bookmark, an embed or a sub-page opens its actions (a click). Side-by-side columns stack. Tables scroll sideways in their frame, the first column held, cells edited in sheets (a month calendar for dates, ticks for choices). Pictures keep their proportion (360 high at most); an uploaded video, PDF or file opens from the web for now. ### Settings (Réglages)
Groups on panels (22) with a hairline edge, grained, in the order they are needed: Serveur, Lecture des liens par Claude, Apparence (theme, layout, "Grain du papier" as a switch), Couleurs and Fond (see Colours and Ground, below), Langue (« Langue de l’interface et des lectures »: Français or English, each named in itself on a segmented track, its help saying it also sets the language Claude writes the links saved from this device in, and that « Relire la page » reads one again in it; on the web the same section also ticks the languages a search by word understands, one at least), Rappels, Données, then the Google Keep import (its steps numbered in ink, a count not a choice). Choosing a language draws every screen again on the same page (`core/i18n`). Help lines stop at about 70 characters; a section's "how it works" is the info card.

### Search and the Spotlight (web)
Search reads notes and pages alike; results replace the wall: pages as rows, then notes as cards, matches in peach. The spotlight (⌘P or Ctrl+P, and Ctrl+K) is a dialog near the top: a matcha-ringed field holding its filters as small matcha tokens; under it the type chips (the chosen one matcha, each with its count) beside the label, date and order menus; then the results, the titles that answer first, then what holds the words in its content with a snippet, each row pressing inside its tab-shaped tint. On a wide screen the chosen result lies beside the list: a note on its own wash, a page's blocks, a link's picture in its 4:5 frame with what Claude read, matches in peach. Its foot names the keys under a fine pointer; on a phone-sized window it is the bottom sheet, its chips and menus one scrolling strip.

### Text Selection and Search Matches
Selected text lies on peach on both clients with a matcha caret and, on the phone, matcha handles. A search match is peach; the web adds an underline in the text's own ink; the phone cannot (React Native 0.88 crashes on a decoration past a cut text's last line).

### The Snip (signature)
The one authored moment, on the slip. While a shared link is read, small scissors work along a dashed cut line from the "Non classé" tray; once filed, the cut runs through in the collection's pigment and its emblem lands: scale 0.4 to 1, rotation -24° to 0, on the `land` spring (phone: damping 12, stiffness 240, mass 0.6; web: `--ease-land`). Reduce Motion turns it into fades.

### Icons
Onde, Carnet's own set, drawn as data in `tools/icons/onde.mjs` on a 24 grid and generated into `glyphs.ts` (`node tools/icons/build_glyphs.mjs`, then `build_android_drawables.mjs` for the widget): 126 glyphs in one ink, neutral on any paper, wash or gradient. Their grammar: one even line (`GLYPH_STROKE`, 1.9) with round ends and joins; every straight stroke longer than a tick takes one soft wave (about 0.6 deep); frames are cushions whose straight sides swell a little and whose round ends stay round; large circles breathe in six slow lobes; dots are solid and follow the wave; arrowheads and chevrons are drawn, their tips softly rounded; letters (B, I, H1...) stay typographic. A form is a stroke and its small filled parts (`GlyphForm`). Eleven glyphs (note, bell, tag, archive, trash, settings, pin, undo, info, sparkle, page) have a solid form, their inner lines cut out of the fill, drawn only when their control is selected. The tag is the label's own cut-out (`LABEL_TAG_PATH`) in one line, pointed at the left with its eyelet and a notch at its right end, so "Labels" and "Créer un label" wear the shape every label wears. A checkbox is a glyph too: the cushion box, ink when open, ink-muted when checked, never matcha. Never coloured, never a face; no icon casts a shadow. A new icon is drawn in `onde.mjs` with its shapes (`wave`, `pillow`, `breathe`, `soft`, `head`), never imported from a library.

### Home-screen Widgets
Pieces of the board laid on the wallpaper, in five widgets (Actions rapides, Classer un lien, Collection, Notes d'un label, Note). A launcher draws them from XML (no path clip, no custom view, no animation), so `tools/widgets/build_widget_resources.mjs` generates everything they wear from `design/`: the `wgen_*` colours (the palette's, day and night), every sheet's continuous corner as four corner vectors and three bands, the pebble's four turns, the emblems, the label tag and the Onde glyphs. They follow the system's day or night, not the app's theme setting.
- **Slabs:** a widget is a slab of the ground at the panel radius (22) with a hairline: the ground's two tones under the web's film grain (`ground-grain`), the one the owner asked for, visible at night where the phone's speckle is not. The one-row Actions rapides is the bottom bar instead: a 56 strip of the ground's foot at the bar radius (15).
- **Sheets:** what lies on a slab 8 in is concentric with it: a note card at 14 on paper or its wash, under the paper speckle at the theme's strength, laid as its own layer so a wash tints the paper and never the grain. The Note widget is the note's own sheet at the panel radius, its done items counted in meta-strong under a hairline. Field strips (Classer un lien 56 high, the tall Actions rapides' search strip 52) take the field colour at the bar radius with the outline edge.
- **Type:** a launcher cannot load an app's fonts. Titles (a collection's, a label's or a note's name, 600 16/22) and the field hints (400 16/23) are Signika set by the app as tinted images (`WidgetType.kt`), each with a plain twin in the system font for when it cannot be drawn and for the picker's previews. List text is the system font at the type scale's sizes and weights: card body 15/21, row title medium 15/20, meta 13/18, text actions 14/18 in deep terracotta.
- **Controls:** a 24 Onde glyph in a 48 target (22 beside a title), its pebble's tint only under the press, given toward round; rows and cards press inside their own shape. The capture pebble is leaf matcha, drawn smaller than the app's: 46 by 42 in the bar (a 52 by 48 target), 40 by 36 in a head (48), 62 by 56 alone in one cell. Checklist rows are 48 high, the whole row ticking its item.
- **Content:** a collection's head is its emblem on its stone (44 by 40), the name with a chevron (the selector), the count, a "+"; link rows keep a 4:5 frame (40 by 50, frame radius 12) and open the link's page in Carnet, a website icon button at their end; a "Voir la collection" foot. Actions rapides: search, list, photo (gallery), camera, link, reminder, and the pebble for a note; narrower sizes drop the rarer actions.
- **The quick window:** a translucent window over the home screen that is its own scrim (fading in 150, decelerate; `SheetScrim` off), with the app's own sheet rising in it: "Classer un lien" (`LinkCaptureSheet`), or a picker as list rows. Once a link is added the sheet closes at once, a slip says where it went (1.6s), and the home screen is back.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** take every radius from `RADII` by role (`SHAPES`, `OUTLINES`; `--cut-*` on the web, `corners(SHAPES.role)` or `outline="kind"` on an `Organic` or `Tappable` on the phone), concentric when nested.
- **Do** keep a control's radius under half its height, so it reads as a soft sheet in every browser.
- **Do** use the pebble for what is chosen, captured or kept and for glimpsed pictures (`data-pebble`, `data-pebble-under`, `pebble` on the phone), each row's neighbours on different turns and a collection's stone on `pebbleTurnOf(id)`.
- **Do** let a pressed pebble give and spring back (90 in, 380 back); keep sheets still.
- **Do** take every colour from `palette.ts` or `backgrounds.ts` through the theme or `var(--…)`, and pass its contrast test.
- **Do** edge cards, chips and bars with the 1px hairline; menus, dialogs, sheet lips and field-like strips with the outline; a control drawn by its edge alone with `edge`.
- **Do** fill actions in terracotta, write links in deep terracotta, and mark capture, the chosen, focus and switches in matcha; keep tonal buttons matcha wash.
- **Do** hover and press inside the control's own shape: 6% of the control's ink under a hovering pointer and 10% under the press (a link-coloured control: 8% and 12%).
- **Do** put grain on the ground and on every sheet at the theme's strength, one layer per surface.
- **Do** lift paper with the shared lifts only: Card at rest, Raised for hover, Float for sheets, dialogs, menus, the drawer and the slip.
- **Do** size a board card from its content with `layoutCard` (`wall.ts`), never by measuring it.
- **Do** keep a link's own picture in its 4:5 frame.
- **Do** settle arrivals on decelerate within 150, 250 or 300ms, and send departures off faster on accelerate.
- **Do** keep Signika alone, set by family on the phone, with the night grade after dark.
- **Do** lay a screen on the ground (`Ground` on the phone, `var(--ground-paint)` on the web), and draw any new icon in Onde (`tools/icons/onde.mjs`).
- **Do** take the primary's and the accent's roles and the ground from the appearance resolved for the theme (`theme.color` and `theme.ground` on the phone, the custom properties on the web); a pinned bar shows the ground's slice (`GroundSlice`), never a flat tone of it.

### Don't:
- **Don't** give an item its own random corners, a seeded outline or bowed sides; every item of a role takes the same radius.
- **Don't** round a control into a capsule or a container past `CORNER_CAP`.
- **Don't** put text on a pebble, or crop a link's own picture to one.
- **Don't** put a stone in a navigation row other than the chosen place's.
- **Don't** float or round the drawer; it runs the full height, square and flush.
- **Don't** paint chrome, text or a whole note in a pigment; notes and stones wear a wash.
- **Don't** use matcha for a filled action or terracotta for selection or focus.
- **Don't** lay a pigment on the matcha fill: a chosen cut-out sits on its own wash or on paper.
- **Don't** bounce anything but the snip's landing and the pebble's spring back.
- **Don't** grow anything from nothing: arrivals start at 0.9 or more.
- **Don't** unmount an overlay before its exit has played.
- **Don't** set uppercase anywhere but the stamp.
- **Don't** draw anything behind an icon's line but its control's pebble tint.
- **Don't** fade a disabled button's pigment or opacity; it sinks to a well.
- **Don't** cast a shadow behind an icon, emblem, stone, swatch, pebble or illustration.
- **Don't** tilt, lift, translate or grow a control under the pointer (static turns such as the stamp, a stone's emblem and the ghost emblem are drawn, not hovers).
- **Don't** stack grain on a nested surface.
- **Don't** colour an icon, give it a face, or bring in an icon library; don't add a third tone to Carnet's own ground, and don't let a colour a person picks reach the screen unfitted.
- **Don't** hard-code a hex, a radius or a shadow in a component; the web reads generated custom properties, the phone reads the theme.
