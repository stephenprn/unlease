# Claude, costs and privacy

What leaves your server, when, and what it costs. Claude is optional: without an API key every app works, and Carnet keeps links as they are, unread. Everything below is Carnet's: it is the only app that asks Claude so far.

Link reading needs an API key from [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com), pasted in the apps' Administration; the key is kept on your server, the only part that talks to Anthropic. You choose the model and a monthly budget (the server stops reading links when it is spent). A link costs roughly one to three cents with the default model, a little more when the server cannot read the page itself and Claude fetches it. What Claude receives: the link's address, what the server read from the page (tags, structured data, an excerpt of its text, one picture) and, when filing, your collections' names and a few titles from each. Your notes and pages are never sent to read a link.

The modules that search the web (promo codes, other shops, second-hand) run only when you open them on a link, in a collection where you switched them on: Claude runs a few web searches (Anthropic's web search tool, $0.01 a search on top of the tokens) with the link's address, name, shop, price and facts, so a search costs more than a reading, roughly ten to twenty-five cents with the default model; the answer is kept on the server for six hours, so opening it again costs nothing. Ratings, a series' recap and other outlets on a story are the same kind of search, kept a week, a month and half a day. A question to Claude sends it the page's text and your question (and searches the web only when you tick it); nothing of it is kept on the server. A recipe's ingredients and method and a copy of an article are read by your server itself (Claude reads a recipe only when its page does not give them). "Y aller" hands the place's name and address to Google Maps, and "SensCritique" the work's title to SensCritique's search, when you press them. Seerr, when you link it in the Administration, is called by your server only, with the API key you give it, which never leaves the server. All of the Claude modules count toward the same monthly budget.

Questions about your pages ("Demander à Claude" on a page) are off until you turn them on in the Administration. Then a quiet button on each page asks Claude about that page, the page with its sub-pages, or all your pages (the closest to the question first, if you allow it): the device sends their text, block by block, with your question and the last few turns of the conversation, and Claude answers from them with each claim marked by the passages it rests on (and searches the web only when you tick it and the Administration allows it). You set how much one question may read (short, medium or long: about 7 000, 20 000 or 67 000 words); a question that reads that much costs about 9, 21 or 61 cents with the default model, a short page a few cents, and a follow-up less, as the pages just read are cached. A page you keep away from Claude (its menu) is never sent, nor anything under it. Nothing of a question is kept on the server, and it counts toward the same monthly budget.

## Who the server and the apps talk to

No analytics, no tracker. The server talks to Anthropic only for the readings, searches and questions above, to the pages it reads for you, and to your S3 storage, your Seafile and your Seerr when you set them. The apps talk to your server; besides it, they load a link's own picture until the server has kept a copy, a video's player when a page embeds one (YouTube's without its cookies, Vimeo, Dailymotion: the only Google service the apps may reach, and only when you play the video), and the sites you open from them.
