# Clock: the home screen and the ringing window

Scope: Clock's home (phone `mobile/clock`, web `web/clock`) and its ringing window (phone). Mode: Operate (the home), with the ringing window as its one Experience-like moment inside the suite's world. Audience: the owner and anyone self-hosting the suite, French or English, an Android phone at the bedside and a desktop browser. Job: see when the next alarm rings and what waits after it, set or change an alarm in a few taps, trust that it rings; as it rings, wake up, see the morning, stop or snooze without fumbling. Constraints: DESIGN.md's world (ground, paper sheets, one continuous corner per role, the pebble only for what is chosen or captured, Signika, matcha chooses, terracotta acts); Material structure; 48 targets.

The direction was dealt by the roll and not answered on the decision page within the wait: built as dealt (the lead), its stated risks met in the build.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home is the week of mornings: seven days across the top, each saying what wakes you, and the chosen morning opened below it with its time, how long until then and the day's first event; it refuses the category's bare list of times as the first thing seen.

OWN-WORLD: The suite's two-tone grainy ground; paper panels at the panel radius with a hairline; the chosen morning on the primary's wash, today's date in the accent's ink; Signika with tabular figures for every time; the second ink for what is off; Safran (waiting) only for a clash; the capture pebble alone at the foot.

STORY: At a glance the visitor knows when the next alarm rings and in how long, and whether tomorrow's first event leaves time; a tap on another morning shows that one; a switch turns an alarm on or off; a card opens it; « Voir la journée » opens Agenda there; « Sonner à 7:00 » fixes a clash in one tap.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The top bar (menu, « Clock »); the strip of seven mornings from today (weekday, date, the time that rings or a dash, a dot when an event starts that day, Safran when it clashes), the next alarm's morning chosen; the morning panel: its day and « dans 9 h 12 » in meta, the time at 56 in Signika SemiBold, the alarm's name, « Ignorer ce matin », a hairline, the first event with its distance from the alarm and « Voir la journée », a clash row when there is one; then « Alarmes », one card per alarm (time at 34, its name over its days, its switch); the capture pebble 16 in from the foot's right.
