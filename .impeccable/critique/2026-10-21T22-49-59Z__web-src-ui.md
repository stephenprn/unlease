## Design specificity

LLM: clearly authored for this product (cut-out emblems, stamps, the snip, grained paper). Risk is the opposite of blandness: "four unequal corners everywhere" turns every short surface into a capsule, so the app reads cute and bubbly rather than clean. The world is right; the corner sizes are too big for it.

Detector: 1 source finding, a false positive (`bounce-easing` on `--ease-land`, the snip's one allowed bounce). Overlay injected on 13 browser runs: 2 real defects (label "Travail" cut to "T.." in a phone-width card footer; table header units at 3.8:1 contrast, pages.module.css:977). 47 hard-coded radii in web CSS vs 95 token uses. No console errors, no overflow at 390px. The detector caught none of the structural problems.

## Overall impression

Warm and authored; the link pipeline is a joy. Two things block "main knowledge app": what is written in a page cannot be found (searching "Alfama", in the Lisbon page, returns "Aucun résultat"), and the shapes read bubbly, not clean. Biggest opportunity: Pages first-class (search, switcher, a page menu) and a calmer corner scale.

## What's working

1. The link pipeline: snip, reading track, plain-words failures with "Réessayer", stamps, "Déjà faits".
2. Trust in Claude's sorting: asks where links go, moves as checkable rows, every sort undoable.
3. Editor fundamentals and shared card maths (layoutCard in core).

## Priority issues

[P0] Pages are invisible to search and there is no way to jump to one. useBoard.ts indexes notes only; typing in search from a page goes to the board. Fix: "Pages" results group above notes (title, path, snippet); Ctrl+P / Cmd+K switcher with recents; "Récentes" on the Pages home. (/impeccable layout + feature work)

[P1] Corner radii turn short things into capsules; notes and pages speak two shape languages. One-line card ~49px tall with 34px corners = pill; same for buttons, fields, search, callouts, selected nav row; facts tables and notices crowd text into curves; pages use 6-11px. Fix: size-based scale in shapes.ts, a guard capping corners at ~30% of height, tokens for the ~47 hard-coded radii. (/impeccable polish)

[P1] Chrome and navigation fight the content. Docked nav jumps 64px between board and other screens and on the first letter of a search (chips row inside the sticky header); chips duplicate the nav's collection list on desktop; pinned chrome ~25% of a phone screen; Pages tree under 7 collection rows pushes Archives/Corbeille/Réglages below the fold; no page menu on the page; deleted pages in a second bin. Fix: drop chips when the nav is docked, header 64 everywhere, chips scroll with the wall on the phone, collapsible nav sections + fixed nav footer, "⋯" page menu in the page head. (/impeccable layout)

[P1] Spacing has no scale; hierarchy inverted in places. Page blocks get 0-4px around boxes, headings 10-22px above; 48px checklist rows with a mouse; 115px link rows (6-7 per desktop screen); composer centred while everything else is left-aligned; bold black counts outweigh descriptions; selected nav row loudest; mentions leave a fake space before punctuation. Fix: 4/8/12/16/24/32/48 rhythm, 12px around boxed blocks, headings 32/28/20/16 above, 36px desktop checklist rows, compact desktop link rows, muted counts. (/impeccable layout)

[P1] Reliability signals too quiet: colour-only sync dot (phone tap gives a snackbar with no action), no saved/synced state on pages. Fix: words beside the dot when not green, "Réglages" action on the phone, "Modifiée à 14:02" in the page head. (/impeccable harden)

## Persona red flags

Hundreds of pages: no full-text search, recents, favourites, backlinks or export; Pages home is a grid of top-level pages only; tree buried under collections and burying Réglages.
