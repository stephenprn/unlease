# Changelog

All notable changes to Unlease are written here: the server and every app share one version. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/2.0.0/) and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). Until 1.0.0, a minor version (0.2.0) may break things: each break is marked **Breaking:** with what to do about it, and [docs/UPGRADING.md](docs/UPGRADING.md) says how to upgrade.

## [Unreleased]

Unlease becomes a suite of apps: Pages leaves Carnet to be an app of its own, on the web as on Android, Contacts and Agenda join them, and the server and its setup are now the suite's, whatever apps it runs.

### Added

- Carnet: « Suivi des prix », a link module a collection switches on in « Modules »: the server reads each product's price once a day from the shop's own page (free), and asks Claude only when the page hides it (at most once a week a link, five a day, never past the monthly budget). The link page says the price before and since when (« Avant 89 € · en baisse depuis le 3 nov. »), when it was last checked, and offers « Vérifier le prix »; a link's row shows how much it dropped (« ↓ 26 € ») or quietly rose.
- Carnet: « Modèles de fiche », on the web and on Android: choose what Claude notes on each kind of link (a recipe's times, a film's director…), each field with its name, its type (text, number, date, length of time, list, yes or no) and what to put in it, starting from the facts Claude noted so far and set back with « Rétablir l'original »; or make your own template (« Vin », « Plante ») and give it to a collection, whose links are then read with it. « Essayer » reads one of your links, or an address you paste, with the fields as they stand and saves nothing (the same fields tried again cost nothing). Once a template changes, its editor and the collection offer to read again the links read with the older version, with what it costs, and a link's page says when it was. From Administration › Claude, a collection's menu (« Modèle de fiche »), a link's menu (« Modifier la fiche ») and the search.
- Carnet: a collection's links can be ordered (the collection's own order, recently added, price low to high or high to low) and filtered (unread, couldn't be read, price dropped), on the web and the phone; each device remembers the choice for each collection. « Tout lire » reads at once the links a collection kept unread while Claude had no key, saying roughly what it costs (« Tout réessayer » for those that failed). A link's row shows your own note, quoted, in place of its facts.
- Carnet: « Annuler » and « Rétablir » in the note editor, as in Keep: step back and forth through what you did to the open note (typing, a step per stretch; checklist items added, removed, ticked, edited or moved; colour and paper, labels, reminder, people, photos, the pin). On the phone they take the date's place once you change something; ⌘Z / ⌘⇧Z work anywhere in the note on the web. A step back syncs like any edit and keeps words another device added meanwhile.
- Carnet: a colour or a paper for several notes at once, from the selection bar (« Couleur et papier »), with « Annuler »; collections take a colour only.
- Agenda: « Aujourd'hui », your day at a glance on the web and on Android: birthdays and all-day events first, then the day in time order with what is on now and what comes next (« Dans 20 min »), Carnet's reminders due today and those missed this week, « Note du jour », the weather, and a look at tomorrow; each row opens the event, the birthday, the note in Carnet or the page in Pages. From the navigation, the drawer or key O; it can be the view Agenda opens on.
- Agenda for Android: an « Aujourd'hui » home-screen widget: at two cells what is on or next, larger the rest of the day with its weather, missed reminders and tomorrow.
- Agenda: « Afficher uniquement celui-ci » shows one calendar alone (on the web in a calendar's ⋮, a right-click or Alt-click on its stone; on the phone a long press in the drawer); « Tout afficher » brings back what showed before, with « Annuler ».
- Contacts on Android: slide a contact to the right to call them, to the left to text them; the slide clicks at its threshold and acts only when let go past it.
- « Souhaiter »: on a contact's birthday and the day after, Contacts (their page, today's birthdays) and Agenda's birthday page write « Joyeux anniversaire, Léa ! » into an SMS, WhatsApp or an e-mail, ready to change and send. In the week before a birthday, the contact's page on Android says when the phone will remind you and turns on « Prévenir la veille »; Agenda's birthday page says the notifications come from Contacts and opens their settings there.
- Pages: a new, empty page offers templates under its title (Journal du jour, Réunion, Voyage, Liste de lecture, Projet), on the web and the phone; a template fills the page with headings, tasks, typed tables and today's date in your language, and one undo takes it back. « Déplacer vers » lists the pages you changed last first.
- Pages on Android: « Réorganiser » puts a page's blocks in order by dragging: open it from a block's actions or by holding the bar's up or down arrow, hold a block and drag it: the other blocks part where it will land (right or left to nest it or take it out); it moves with what it holds, and each move can be undone.
- Search finds the settings, in every app on the web and on Android: typing « grain », « budget », « S3 », « clé », « fond » or « rappels » offers the place in Réglages or the Administration that holds it, above the results (and in the spotlight), opened at its section. French and English words both work.

Day, three days, week, month, year and schedule views; events made with a click or a drag and moved or resized by dragging on the web, with the + button or a long press on Android; all-day and multi-day events; repeating events (every day, workday, week, month on a date or on « the second Tuesday », year, or your own rule, until a day or a number of times), each occurrence changed or deleted alone, with the following ones, or all; reminders; a time zone per event; a place (opened in Google Maps or OpenStreetMap, as you pick each time, or on Android in the phone's maps app you choose, with the day's weather), guests from your contacts (their faces, opening them in Contacts), files kept with the event (in Seafile under `Agenda/`), a web link (its tracking removed, as Carnet's links), a colour, labels; times typed as you say them on the web (« 20h », « 2030 ») or picked from the quarters, the end's with how long the event lasts; a description that mentions pages, Carnet's notes and links, people and other events. Duplicate an event, a bin, a search and the spotlight on the web; the views side by side in the web's bar, and the hours opening on the morning, or on the time it is.
- Agenda shows your contacts' birthdays (their age, and « Ouvrir dans Contacts », WhatsApp, a message or a call when they have a number, their gift ideas from Carnet's « Cadeaux » and « Ajouter une idée »), Carnet's notes with a reminder, and the days written in your pages; each is a layer you show or hide.

Import Google Agenda's export (an `.ics` from Takeout, or one shared to the phone), export your own events, and publish them at a secret address another calendar subscribes to (Administration, Agenda, « Publication »).
- Type an event in a few words in Agenda (« Dîner chez Paul jeudi 20h », "Lunch with Sam at Pizzeria tomorrow 1pm"), read on the device in French and English, by Claude when it needs to be; ask Claude about your calendar (Administration, Agenda, « Questions à Claude »).
- Reminders as notifications on Android, and in the browser as push notifications even with the tab closed (Agenda's Réglages).

- Pages and Carnet's formatted notes mention events (type `@`); an event lists the pages and notes that mention it, and a page, a note or a contact lists the events that mention them (« Vus ensemble » on a contact: the events they were a guest of).
- « Créer une page de notes » on an event makes a page in Pages with its date, its guests and the event mentioned; « Note du jour » makes (or opens) the day's page under « Journal ».
- An « Agenda » block in Pages: the coming events (of one label, if you like) in a page.
- « Ajouter à l'agenda », a module of Carnet's collections: Claude finds an event's date, time and place in a saved link (a concert, a show, a booking) and Agenda's form opens with them.

- **Contacts**, a Google Contacts of your own: its web app at `/contacts` and its APK on Android (`contacts-vX.Y.Z.apk`), with its own icon (a card holding someone, on the suite's pebble). Each person keeps a first and last name, a company and a job, phone numbers, e-mails and postal addresses (each with its kind or a name of your own), a birthday (with or without the year), a note, a photo and labels; favourites come first and the coming birthdays are listed. Call, text, e-mail or open WhatsApp on their number from their page (on Android also a WhatsApp call, when the contacts permission is given); each address lies on its own card with a little plan drawn for it (no map is fetched) and opens in Google Maps or OpenStreetMap, as you pick each time (on Android in the phone's maps app you choose, or as you pick), as do the directions to it. Labels, a bin, a search that finds numbers however they were typed, and the spotlight on the web. A server that never chose its apps runs it at once; one whose apps were chosen in the setup or Administration turns it on there (« Applications »).

On Android it also imports the phone's own contacts, and takes a contact shared to it (WhatsApp's « Partager le contact »). It exports everyone, or a selection, as a `.vcf`.
- Contacts' home-screen widgets on Android: quick calls (your favourites, or the people of a label, one tap to call) and « Nouveau contact ».
- Contacts finds duplicates (the same e-mail, number or full name) and merges them in one tap: the one kept gains what the others held, the others wait in the bin, and a mention of them names the one kept. A group can be left as it is.
- Birthday notifications on Android, on the day (and the day before, if you like) at the hour you choose, with « Appeler » and « Message » in the notification; turned on in Contacts' Réglages.
- An address opens in your own maps app on Android (Organic Maps, OsmAnd, Google Maps…), and on the web in the maps you pick in Réglages (OpenStreetMap by default, Google Maps or Apple's Plans).

Once chosen, an address, its directions and an event's place open there at once; asked, the app lists the phone's own maps apps by name and icon, OpenStreetMap and « Copier l’adresse ».
- Gift ideas: « Idée cadeau » on a contact (or on their birthday in Agenda) keeps a product's link in Carnet's « Cadeaux » collection (made the first time, found again after), with the contact named among the link's people; Claude reads it as any saved link, and the birthday lists it among their ideas.
- People on Carnet's notes and links: a note (« Ajouter », « Personnes ») or a link (« Personnes » on its page) names the people of Contacts it is for or about. They show as chips opening the person in Contacts, as faces on the note's card and by name on the link's row; Carnet's search finds a note by their names, and the contact's « Mentionné dans » lists the notes and links that name them.

It still shows Carnet's notes, collections and links in its pages, and opens them in Carnet (its web app, or its phone app when installed).

The question says how many links Claude will read and roughly what it costs.

- A background for a drawing in Pages: it lies on plain paper by default, on the web as on Android (on the web it lay on the page itself), and « Fond » in its tools lays it on a colour's wash instead, or on nothing (« Sans fond », the page showing through); synced with the page and kept in its undo. - Android: each app's icon in the launcher takes its primary colour, a palette's own or, for a colour picked precisely, the nearest palette's. It changes once you leave the app after changing colours; the launcher may then take the app's shortcut off the home screen (add it back from the app list), and the app leaves Recents once.

### Security

- Release builds of the phone apps never fall back to React Native's public debug key: without the upload key a release build stops, and a GitHub release fails before publishing anything, then checks each APK's signing certificate (never a debug key; the repository variable `ANDROID_SIGNING_CERT_SHA256` when set).
- The release workflows pin the third-party actions that run beside the signing key or publish the release (`android-actions/setup-android` v4.0.4, `softprops/action-gh-release` v2.6.2) to commit SHAs.
- The brake on wrong tokens no longer believes `CF-Connecting-IP` or `X-Real-IP` from any client: it counts per connection address, or per the one header `UNLEASE_TRUSTED_PROXY_HEADER` names. The right token always passes, the addresses it remembers are capped, and refused guesses no longer write one log line each.
- Carnet's web app no longer saves anything when its capture or share address is opened: a page that sent you there could add links or notes without a click. Shared links now open « Classer un lien » prefilled, and shared text asks before becoming a note.
- Calls to Claude started together can no longer spend past the monthly budget: each request is held at its worst against the budget until its cost is recorded.
- Pages: blocks put on the clipboard by another website are no longer taken as Pages' own copy (they paste as their HTML or text), and pasted links keep only web, mail and telephone addresses.
- Notifee's notification activity is no longer exported in Carnet, Contacts and Agenda: other apps can no longer send them fake notification taps.
- A launcher could tick or untick any checklist item through Carnet's Note widget: only an item the widget shows is ticked. Contacts' quick-call widget calls only the number the app holds for a face it shows, and Agenda's widgets open only the suite's own pages.
- A phone app reads a sibling's connection only from that sibling's own package, signed with the same key.
- Agenda: a subscribed calendar, the public holidays or an imported `.ics` can no longer slip links or mentions into an event's description, and an event's address is kept only when it is a web address; Agenda opens links only to the web, mail or a phone number.
- The published calendar (`/ical/<secret>.ics`) names only contacts and the events it publishes; any other mention (notes, links, pages, private ones and those in the bin included) reads « … ».
- The Android apps keep nothing in Android's backups or in a transfer to a new phone (the token included): a new phone connects again and syncs.
- The web apps' Content Security Policy no longer lets them reach `http://localhost` or `http://127.0.0.1`.
- `/api/health` no longer tells a caller without the token which release the server runs.
